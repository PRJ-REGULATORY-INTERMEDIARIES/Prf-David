# RGAM v0.6.1 — Primary-source verification matrix

**Date:** 3 October 2026. This matrix records the *actual scope of accessible reading during this revision*, not a certification that all references have been audited. Distinguish primary text, accessible publisher excerpts, official abstract, publisher description, analytical fichamento, and metadata. Full-text verification of a book is not inferred from a chapter or summary. No external source is an authority for the new I1–I4 S→A decision rule, which is the author's provisional synthesis.
