## Authority and attribution

**Authorized by Igor in the existing record.** The historical decision log records, among other matters, the distinction between instruments as relation characteristics, juridically constituted devices and materially instantiated devices; the distinction between accountability as a relation and as a qualifier; the separation of documentary inscription, legal constitution, material instantiation and observed operation; claim-level evidence status; external-reference discipline; and the separation of operational-only arrangements from the primary legal architecture. These existing decisions remain intact.

**Recommendations incorporated prospectively.** The six ADJ directions above guide only this package's wording and proposed next questions. They do not confer a new status on the ontology, change the frozen v0.6.1 manuscript, or turn a machine-produced pilot judgment into human adjudication.

**Reserved for David.** The consultation asks David to judge: (i) whether RGAM's property-bearer distinctions complement V8 or add avoidable ontological weight; (ii) what criterion should govern architecture boundaries across instruments; and (iii) whether and how a provisional regime notation G should be developed in light of V8's architecture-among-regimes account.

## Empirical and reliability gate

GDPR Pilot 01 remains `MACHINE_PROVISIONAL`, generated by a single evaluator. Its legal illustration is therefore not independent validation and no record is relabelled `HUMAN_ADJUDICATED`. Full GDPR validation is not a prerequisite for a preliminary conceptual consultation; it is a prerequisite for stronger empirical or reliability claims.

Before such claims for an expanded corpus, independent double coding should be conducted with appropriate unit matching before selecting statistics. Agreement must be assessed separately for: (1) normative assignments, (2) qualified governance relationships, and (3) higher-order subconfigurations/architectures. A statistic such as Krippendorff's alpha may be appropriate only after the units, categories, missingness treatment and matching procedure are justified; one aggregate score would obscure disagreement across interpretive levels. Human legal adjudication, boundary sensitivity and external-source verification remain later work.

## Open scientific incompatibility

No decisive incompatibility has been established. The live risk is conceptual narrowing: a legal-documentary morphology could be mistaken for an exhaustive theory of governance, despite V8's expressly broader regime conception. A secondary risk is redundancy: V8 may already accommodate the proposed disciplines without a separate ontology. Both risks are deliberately put to David rather than resolved by assertion.
