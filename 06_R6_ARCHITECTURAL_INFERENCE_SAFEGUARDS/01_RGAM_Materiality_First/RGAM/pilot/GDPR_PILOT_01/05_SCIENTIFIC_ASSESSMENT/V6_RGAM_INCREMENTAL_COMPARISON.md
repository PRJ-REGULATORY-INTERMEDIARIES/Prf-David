# V6 vs RGAM — what the GDPR evidence shows about incremental value

**Status:** `MACHINE_PROVISIONAL` assessment by the same agent that applied RGAM; **not** an independent evaluation. V6 is cited from the full reading of the DOCX in an earlier session (section numbers as in V6); it was not re-read in this session. Stratmann et al. (2026) and ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 are known only through the accessible abstract, the supplied analytical notes and the v0.6.1 verification matrix; **no comparison with their methods is made on evidence**, because the pilot produced no diagram or description and their full texts were not read.

**Principle (directive Part VII):** an unsuccessful or redundant RGAM distinction is a legitimate finding. More fields, nodes or diagrams are not progress.

## 2. Where RGAM did not help or created difficulty

1. **Relation types.** V6's ten types did not fit peer cooperation, consultation, escalation, jurisdictional and liability allocation; 44.5% of relations ended up in one type (UNC-006). RGAM did not repair this.
2. **Self-regulatory structure.** The relation-pivotal design leaves 35 regulated-side assignments outside any S; V6's own unit (with an Actor B) shares the limit.
3. **Position ontology.** "Legally specified place" does not fit roles defined by conduct (TP-01).
4. **DEV threshold.** Ten flags.
5. **S discovery** depended on analyst-defined predicates (false positives found).

## 3. What the pilot supports and does not support

- **Supported (provisionally, for this instrument):** C2–C3, C5 (substantive visibility); C4 (new strictness); a usable closure rule (C8).
- **Redundant or not distinguishable from a careful V6 application:** most of C1; most of C6 conclusions (W≠A, non-exclusivity).
- **Unproven:** reliability of the accountability criteria and of I2/I3 thresholds; value of the contrast design, devices as a category beyond registers, status axes, and multi-view output.
- **Not assessed:** Stratmann- or ISO-based comparison.

## 4. Next test that would be informative

An independent coder applying V6 alone to the same text, then RGAM, on a stratified sample of provisions, with disagreements adjudicated by a legal specialist: the only way to say whether RGAM changes the result or merely documents it.
