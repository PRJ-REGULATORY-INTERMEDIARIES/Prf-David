**Label collision to avoid.** In the frozen ontology, "I1/I2/I3" are *instrument situations*. In the manuscript and in Igor's directive, "I1–I4" are the *S→A warrant conditions*. In all Pilot 01 files **I1–I4 mean the S→A inference conditions**. Instrument situations are written **INS-TYPE** (typed characteristic of an assignment or relation), **DEV** (juridically constituted institutional device) and the establishment status **MEST** (materially established, evidenced beyond the legal prescription).
