# Research design — GDPR Pilot 01

**Status:** exploratory empirical application of RGAM (v0.6.1 as provisional authority), executed 2026-10-03 by an AI agent under Igor Caires Machado's direction. Single evaluator; machine-provisional; **not** validation.

## Research question
What regulatory governance architectures and potentially overlapping subconfigurations can be reconstructed from the GDPR examined as a complete, predefined legal instrument? Secondary: which relations organise governance work; how authority, monitoring, intermediation and accountability are distributed; which configurations cross chapters; when subconfigurations may be components of an integrated architecture; which properties are demonstrable or indeterminate; whether RGAM adds inferential distinctions beyond V6.

## Design
1. **Corpus (W):** the whole instrument, fixed text and version (CORPUS_AND_VERSION_REGISTER). No provision was chosen for expected architectural content.
2. **Frozen rules before extraction:** `DECISION_RULES.md` v1.0; later changes logged prospectively (v1.1, v1.2). Alignment of v0.6.1 with the frozen ontology: `ONTOLOGY_ALIGNMENT_AND_PRECEDENCE.md`.
3. **Full coverage:** every one of the 389 paragraph units screened in article order; point-level inventory automatic (382 points).
4. **Extraction in article-order batches** (Chapters I→XI), with separate records for normative assignments, positions/devices, qualified relations and external references; N1–N4 applied by wording.
5. **Reconstruction:** candidate subconfigurations by explicit predicates over extracted records; S→A tested with I1–I4 and the anti-circularity sequence; outcomes limited to integrated / distinguishable / overlapping or partially integrated / indeterminate.
6. **Trace selection by pre-fixed rule** (DR §17): seeded draw among S citing ≥2 chapters, plus the deterministic largest-support candidate.
7. **Comparison with V6** on the same evidence (not on a separate sample).
8. **Human adjudication queue**; blind human coding **not** done (stated).

## What was not done
No machine-coding pipeline beyond this single run; no inter-coder reliability; no review of recitals; no retrieval of external instruments; no implementation or operation evidence; no discretion coding; no graph metrics or diagrams; no change to the manuscript or the frozen ontology; no Git operation; no parallel agents.

## Validity limits
(i) The S predicates and the I3 consequence statements were written by the agent that extracted the data. (ii) The unit of screening is the paragraph. (iii) The baseline text is a historical authentic text. (iv) Outcomes are provisional and not independently reproduced.
