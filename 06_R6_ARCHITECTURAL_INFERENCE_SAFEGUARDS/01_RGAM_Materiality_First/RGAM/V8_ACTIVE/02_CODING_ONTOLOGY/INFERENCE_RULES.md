# Inference rules

1. **Text to statement:** split a provision only where distinct subjects, actions, modalities, objects, conditions or consequences warrant separate institutional propositions.
2. **Statement to relationship:** require identifiable parties plus a governance-relevant action, obligation, power, resource or dependency. Otherwise retain a normative assignment.
3. **Relationship to configuration:** state a theoretical pattern and the elements/attributes needed. A single report does not establish a reporting system without evidence of receiving, assessment or follow-up where that is claimed.
4. **Configuration to architecture:** specify problem and scale, qualified coupling, configurational consequence, alternative groupings and boundary limitations. Frequency, common terminology, shared actors, visual adjacency and graph connectivity are insufficient alone.
5. **Architecture to regime:** make a separate regime-boundary decision. A statute or a connected graph is not equivalent to a regime.
6. **Stopping rule:** stop at the highest level actually supported; record `deferred` or `human_review_needed` rather than promote a claim.
7. **Sensitivity:** test credible alternative boundary, constituent or relationship interpretations when a whole-level claim depends on them. Removal is optional and cannot be used as a universal existence rule.

These are operational specifications and methodological hypotheses built around V8's inferential method; they are not quoted authorial thresholds.
