# Regime relations model

`regime_members` implements many-to-many membership: a legal unit, relationship, configuration or architecture may contribute to several candidate regimes. `regime_relations` records a source-supported relation between two regimes with `relation_type`, `scope`, `evidence`, `inference_level`, `alternative_interpretation` and `status`.

Permitted relation values are: `nested`, `embedded`, `layered`, `overlapping`, `cross_cutting`, `coupled`, `constitutively_linked`, `meta_regulatory`, `regime_complex`, `latent_functional`, and `undetermined`. They are descriptions to be warranted, not a mutually exclusive exhaustive taxonomy.

A latent or functional regime needs a declared problem/function, relatively stable roles, structured relationships, decision or standard-setting arrangements where relevant, monitoring/verification/enforcement/review where relevant, continuity and sufficient coherence. No one feature, including a lexical label, establishes it.
