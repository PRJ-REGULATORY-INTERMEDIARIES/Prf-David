# V8 analytical structure

| Level | Constructs | Role in the workflow |
|---|---|---|
| Legal evidence | legal texts, provisions, institutional statements | Documentary substrate and provenance. |
| Level I | objects; actors; functions; mechanisms; instruments; relationships | Directly observable or closely text-anchored governance elements. |
| Level II | authority; discretion; accountability | Relational attributes requiring evidence-sensitive interpretation. |
| Level III | strategies; architectures; regimes; governance logics | Higher-order constructs reconstructed from configurations. |

## Distinctions preserved

- **Function** asks what governance work is done.
- **Mechanism** asks how an actor or arrangement influences conduct, capacity, information, choice or decision.
- **Instrument** is the concrete legal or institutional device through which organization occurs.
- **Relationship** identifies how actors are legally connected; it is typed, not a generic edge.

V8's legal-governance inference ladder is: legal text → institutional statements → governance relationships → governance configurations → legal architectures → governance regimes. Each transition is inferential, and the burden of inference increases as analysis moves away from the text. Primary coding records lower-level evidence; it does not label a provision as an entire architecture, regime or logic.

The higher-order constructs retained here are **regulatory strategies**, **governance architectures**, **regulatory regimes** and **governance logics**. They can overlap or coexist; none is an automatic provision-level classification.

## Regime relations

Candidate regimes may be nested (containment), embedded (location/dependence), layered (historical addition), overlapping (shared jurisdiction), cross-cutting (functional reach), coupled (dependence), meta-regulatory, constitutively linked, part of a regime complex, or latent/functional. These are candidate higher-order interpretations; membership is many-to-many and neither a statute nor a graph cluster determines it.
