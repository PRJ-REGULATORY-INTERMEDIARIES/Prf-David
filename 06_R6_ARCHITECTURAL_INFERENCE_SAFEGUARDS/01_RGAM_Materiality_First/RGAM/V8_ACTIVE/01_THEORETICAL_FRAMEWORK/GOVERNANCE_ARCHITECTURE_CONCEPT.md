# Governance architecture concept

## V8 definition and implications

V8 defines governance architecture as the legally constituted structural configuration through which governance work, authority, discretion and accountability are distributed among actors and organized through relationships, mechanisms and instruments. The definition makes architecture a property of organized relations, not a synonym for a legal act, an actor inventory, a diagram, or a regulatory regime.

Architectures are multidimensional. A legal arrangement can be centralized in enforcement, intermediated in verification, self-regulatory in implementation, multilevel in jurisdiction and polycentric in decision capacity. Hierarchy, delegation, self-regulation, meta-regulation, assurance, networked governance and polycentricity are therefore recurrent configurations, not exclusive labels that must characterize an entire instrument.

## Project interpretation

The project treats a proposed architecture as a bounded claim. It must state the governance problem, domain, temporal interval, scale, constituent evidence and boundary rule. A documentary corpus is an evidentiary universe, not proof that it contains one integrated architecture. A graph is an analytic representation and cannot itself settle legal connection, authority or institutional consequence.

The resulting claim is about the formal legal arrangement observable from the selected sources. It is distinct from claims about whether institutions were established, how they implemented duties, or how governance operated in practice.
