# Executive research brief

The active RGAM workspace now operates solely from V8. It includes a V8-derived ontology, a traceable relational model, a fresh exploratory GDPR application and a consultation interface.

The methodological hypothesis is modest: explicitly identifying relations, configurations, property bearers, boundaries and alternatives may make architectural claims more transparent. The preliminary GDPR illustration finds a qualified cross-border cooperation-and-consistency arrangement; it does not validate the method or establish a single architecture for the Regulation.
