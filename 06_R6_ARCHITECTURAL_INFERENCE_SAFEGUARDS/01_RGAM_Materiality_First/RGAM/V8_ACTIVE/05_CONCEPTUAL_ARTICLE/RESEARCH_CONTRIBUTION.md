# Proposed research contribution

The proposed contribution is an evidence-disciplined morphological method for operationalizing V8's relational reconstruction. Its candidate additions are: (1) explicit separation of legal units, statements, elements, assignments, qualified relationships, configurations and claims; (2) property-bearer discipline; (3) a traceable configuration-to-architecture register; (4) boundary and sensitivity records; and (5) many-to-many regime modeling.

It does **not** claim to originate relational governance analysis, multidimensional architecture, overlap, regime architecture or legal architectural visualization. Its distinct value remains a hypothesis until independent users can show that the method improves same-evidence reconstruction.

## Collaboration priorities

**Coordination:** the method supplies a non-destructive interoperability layer. It links provision-based institutional statements to qualified relationships and higher-order claims through separate mappings, so that it can be compared with existing relational-leximetric coding without altering original codebooks.

**Theoretical innovation:** the contribution is not a new display of V8 categories. It asks whether decomposition, interfaces, situated positions, part–whole attribution, boundary/scale declaration and configurational comparison change a conclusion reached from the same legal material.

**Practical progress:** the GDPR trace makes the procedure concrete: a source-linked sequence moves from legal statements and qualified relations to a deliberately bounded architectural implication, while retaining uncertainty rather than forcing an instrument-wide type.
