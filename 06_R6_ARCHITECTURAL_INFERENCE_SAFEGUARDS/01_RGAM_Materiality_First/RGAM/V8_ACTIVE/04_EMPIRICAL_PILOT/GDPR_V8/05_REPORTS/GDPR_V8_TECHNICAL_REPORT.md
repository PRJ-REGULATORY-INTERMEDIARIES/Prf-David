# GDPR V8 technical report

## Scope and source

This is a new exploratory V8-oriented reconstruction from Regulation (EU) 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679, using verified EUR-Lex official-source renderings. The historical base is OJ L 119 (4 May 2016), with the verified OJ L 127 (23 May 2018) corrigendum applied. The pilot does not assert that this is the current consolidated GDPR; later changes are unverified.

## Actual coverage achieved

The deterministic parser materialized **99 articles, 389 paragraph units and 382 point units**, plus 173 recitals. The coverage register includes every parsed operative paragraph and point; all were machine-first screened. It contains 545 lexical candidate statements. This is complete **machine screening**, not complete expert legal interpretation. Curated V8 coding covers targeted structural arrangements and is explicitly limited to 23 source-linked observations and 13 qualified relationships.

## Direct observations and preliminary reconstruction

The curated records identify controller/processor arrangements, internal compliance/DPO safeguards, code and certification prompts, national supervisory authority provisions, cross-border cooperation and consistency, complaint/judicial review and sanction-related materials. They preserve institutional statements and do not generate a relationship merely from a duty.

The detailed trace supports a narrowly framed preliminary claim about cross-border cooperation and consistency. Articles 56 and 60 allocate and sequence cooperation among national supervisory authorities. Articles 60(4), 63 and 65 conditionally escalate a qualifying disagreement to a Board binding decision; Article 65(6) preserves the lead authority's final decision addressed to the controller or processor. The defensible result is a formal, configuration-level description of distributed first-instance authority and concentrated dispute-stage authority, qualified by opinion/standstill and urgency routes. It is not a claim about the whole Regulation.

## Limits and next validation step

National implementing acts, external institutional bases, later amendments, Treaty review and implementation/operation are outside the declared corpus. Accountability and discretion are not fully coded across the pilot. Candidate configurations have not been independently discovered or double-coded. The next step is a blind, stratified legal review of the full candidate statement register, followed by separate agreement assessment for statements/assignments, relationships, attributes, configurations and architectural claims.

## Practical-progress demonstration

The pilot demonstrates the intended workflow rather than a finished classification: legal statements in Articles 56, 60, 63 and 65 are separated from the functions, mechanisms and instruments they support; their qualified relationships are retained across provisions; and only then is a narrow configuration-level architectural implication advanced. The same trace distinguishes an interface (conditional referral), a situated position (the Board at the qualifying dispute stage) and a part–whole claim (staged authority distribution). Thus the architectural-method layer is tested against actual legal text without forcing a whole-instrument architecture.
