# V8 conceptual baseline

## Authorial propositions in V8

V8 treats law as an institutional technology that structures governance: it identifies objects, creates or recognizes actors, allocates functions, activates mechanisms, establishes instruments and relationships, and distributes authority, discretion and accountability. Governance architecture is the legally constituted configuration through which these governing capacities and relationships are organized. It is a configurational and multidimensional object, not an exclusive type inferred from one actor, instrument, provision, or frequency count.

V8 distinguishes three analytical levels. **Level I** contains observable governance elements: objects, actors, functions, mechanisms, instruments and relationships. **Level II** contains relational attributes: authority, discretion and accountability. **Level III** contains higher-order constructs: strategies, architectures, regimes and governance logics. The relationship is the pivotal bridge between textual observations and higher-order institutional inference.

V8 requires separate units of coding, analysis and inference. Legal text and institutional statements are evidentiary inputs; relationships and their attributes are substantive analytical objects; configurations, architectures and regimes are progressively higher-order inferences. Higher-order claims require explicit aggregation rules, configurational significance rather than simple prevalence, vertical traceability and a stopping rule.

## Regimes, architectures and operation

Regulatory regimes are broader institutional systems than architectures. They can include law, organizations, standards, decision procedures, knowledge practices and public or non-state actors. Regimes may be nested, embedded, layered, overlapping, cross-cutting, coupled, meta-regulatory or latent/functional. V8 distinguishes architecture **within** regimes from architecture **among** regimes; multiple membership is possible and regime boundaries are inferential questions, not statutory defaults.

V8 also distinguishes formal legal architecture from implementation architecture and operational architecture. Formal reconstruction does not establish implementation, actual practice or effectiveness.

## Operational choices made in this workspace

The following are project specifications, not propositions attributed to V8: a normalized data model; controlled coding fields; explicit provenance links; a configuration and inference register; a regime-relation table; a boundary/sensitivity register; and an architectural-method layer that asks analysts to identify the bearer of a claimed property. These specifications remain revisable and are visibly marked as such in the codebook.
