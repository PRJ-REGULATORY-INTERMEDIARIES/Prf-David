# Open scientific decisions

1. **Function–mechanism–instrument boundaries.** V8 distinguishes them but leaves some taxonomy under development. The codebook retains an `uncertain_boundary` state rather than forcing a final category.
2. **Relationship threshold.** A normative assignment without an evidenced governance-relevant counterparty remains an institutional statement/assignment; it is not converted into a graph edge.
3. **Configuration and architecture boundaries.** The project specifies a transparent inference protocol, but its thresholds require human assessment and future testing.
4. **Regime inference.** Multiple membership and regime relations are modeled; no pilot regime is claimed solely from textual connectedness or a statute boundary.
5. **Authority, discretion and accountability.** Their components are coded separately where evidence permits. Reporting alone is not accountability; a broader reliability exercise is necessary.
6. **Current-law status.** The GDPR source is a historically fixed baseline. Later legal developments are unverified.
7. **Validation.** Independent double coding, matched units and level-specific agreement must precede reliability claims.
