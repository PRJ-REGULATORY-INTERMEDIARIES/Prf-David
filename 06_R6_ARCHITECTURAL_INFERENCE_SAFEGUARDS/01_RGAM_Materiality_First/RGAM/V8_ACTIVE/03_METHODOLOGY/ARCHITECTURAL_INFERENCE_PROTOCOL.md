# Architectural inference protocol

For every candidate architectural claim, complete the following register.

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| Scope | What problem, domain, jurisdiction, period and legal-text boundary is at issue? |
| Configuration | Which stated configuration(s), elements and relations support the claim? |
| Coupling | What qualified legal connection joins the relevant components? |
| Consequence | What distribution, sequence, dependency, alternative route or other property arises from the arrangement? Who bears it? |
| Significance | Why does frequency, authority, centrality, exclusivity, sequence, consequence or substitutability support the claim? |
| Alternatives | What rival grouping or interpretation is plausible? |
| Sensitivity | Would an alternative boundary, unresolved source or relation reading change the claim? |
| Stopping point | Is the warranted outcome a relation, configuration, provisional architecture, regime implication, or deferred claim? |

The output may be `supported within declared scope`, `partially supported`, `undetermined`, or `not supported`. It must never convert uncertainty into an architecture label.
