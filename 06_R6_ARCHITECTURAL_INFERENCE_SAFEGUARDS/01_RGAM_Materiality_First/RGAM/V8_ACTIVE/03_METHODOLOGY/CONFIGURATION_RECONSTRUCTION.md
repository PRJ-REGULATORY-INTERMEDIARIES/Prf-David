# Configuration reconstruction

A candidate configuration is a scoped combination of elements, qualified relationships and attributes organized around a governance problem, function or stage. It has a stated question, membership rule, legal-unit evidence, possible overlap and uncertainty record. Editorial location in a statute is not a membership rule.

Candidate configurations may be overlapping, nested, sequential, alternative or complementary. A shared actor, a common label or connected network cannot alone establish that they form a larger architecture. The project checks whether relations provide a qualified procedural, conditional, authority, information, review or other institutionally supported coupling.

Positional analysis may ask whether a relation or actor is a necessary stage, alternative route, bottleneck, authority gateway or peripheral support. Such a property needs legal meaning and a declared corpus coverage; graph reachability by itself is not information flow, legal dependency or indispensability.
