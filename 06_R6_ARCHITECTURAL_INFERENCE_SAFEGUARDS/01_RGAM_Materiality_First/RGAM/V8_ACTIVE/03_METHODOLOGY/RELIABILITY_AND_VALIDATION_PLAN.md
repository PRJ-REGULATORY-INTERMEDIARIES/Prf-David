# Reliability and validation plan

Validation is level-specific. Documentary checks ask whether source/version and legal-unit identification are correct. Statement checks ask whether subjects, actions, modalities and conditions are parsed consistently. Relation checks ask whether the legally constituted connection is represented accurately. Configuration and architecture checks ask whether the stated inferential rule and evidence warrant the claim. External validation concerns divergence between formal reconstruction and implementation/operation.

Before any reliability claim, a future study should use independent double coding of a stratified sample, including negative screens and ambiguous units. Unit matching must be established before choosing statistics. Agreement should be reported separately for institutional statements/normative assignments, qualified relationships, attributes, configurations and architectural claims. A statistic such as Krippendorff's alpha is not a substitute for justified units, categories and adjudication rules. No statistic is reported in this workspace.

Human legal review is particularly needed for cross-provision relations, discretion, accountability components, constituent boundaries, external dependencies and higher-order classifications.
