# Extraction protocol

1. Verify the documentary source, version, language, hashes and corrigenda before interpretation.
2. Materialize legal units with stable article/paragraph/point identifiers. Treat recitals separately: they provide context but are not automatically operative evidence.
3. Screen every operative unit and record status, candidate categories, uncertainty and reason for exclusion/deferment.
4. Extract institutional statements from relevant units. Preserve exact source text and modality.
5. Code elements separately: object, actor/position, function, mechanism, instrument and relationship. A statement can yield multiple elements.
6. Record normative assignments where a counterpart is absent, unresolved or not governance-relevant. Never fabricate a counterpart to complete a graph.
7. Reconstruct cross-provision relations only with explicit evidence links to every necessary statement.
8. Attach authority, discretion and accountability components separately and retain uncertainty.
9. Defer strategies, configurations, architectures, logics and regimes until lower-level evidence is available.

The first pass in this pilot is machine-assisted lexical screening. It is transparent, reproducible and deliberately not treated as legal interpretation or validation.
