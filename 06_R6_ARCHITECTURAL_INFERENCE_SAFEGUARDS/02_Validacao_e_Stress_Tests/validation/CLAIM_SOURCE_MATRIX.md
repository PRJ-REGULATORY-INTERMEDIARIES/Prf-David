## Immediate audit conclusion

The strongest defensible contribution is a proposed, traceable synthesis that connects text-level legal coding to relationship-level analysis and explicitly limits architectural inference. The current V6 wording periodically upgrades this into a general discovery or demonstrated capability. The revisions should lower novelty and empirical-capability language until primary comparisons and a real-law demonstration are available.
