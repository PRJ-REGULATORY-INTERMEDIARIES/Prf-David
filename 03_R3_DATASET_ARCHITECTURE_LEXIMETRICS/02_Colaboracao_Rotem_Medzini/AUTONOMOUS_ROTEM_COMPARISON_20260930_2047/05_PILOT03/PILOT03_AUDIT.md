## 2. Provenance qualifications (new in this run)

### Q1 — The "two independent model passes" were generated by script
**Fact.** Both coding passes are produced by `build_pilot02.py` / `build_pilot03.py`:
- The calibration rows are hard-coded lists (`BASE = [...]` in `build_pilot02.py`).
- The blind-challenge rows come from one hard-coded tuple list.
- Pass B differs from pass A only through a scripted override on five provisions (`if variant == "B" and pid in {"C01_PR04","C02_PR03","C03_PR02","C04_PR02","C05_PR03"}`), with the note "Boundary interpretation intentionally retained for blind comparison".

**What the reports say.**
- The PILOT_02 report states this honestly: "simulated model independence".
- The PILOT_03 reports say "two independent source-first model passes" (`PILOT_03_REPORT.md` l.14; `PILOT_03_FINAL_REPORT.md` l.23; protocol l.21).

**Consequences.**
- The 245/264 agreement figure is **not an empirical reliability estimate**.
- The 19 disagreements are **constructed calibration questions**.
- The human decisions remain genuine human answers to those questions, and they are preserved as such.

### Q2 — The final gate is a constant in the code
`incorporate_human_adjudication.py` hard-codes `FINAL_GATE = "ONTOLOGY_STABLE_WITH_REVISIONS"` and the backward-impact text. Re-running therefore reproduces the gate by construction. It is a **recorded researcher-level judgment**, not a computed test outcome.

### Q3 — Excerpt fidelity
The 33 "legal excerpts" are hard-coded strings, and all of them are labelled `DIRECT_PROVISION_VERIFIED`. This run checked each one against the official text it fetched (`PILOT03_EXCERPT_VERIFICATION.csv`):

| Sample | Verbatim (all fragments) | Verbatim (some fragments) | Close paraphrase | Not located verbatim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calibration (17, P01–P05) | 12 | 4 | 1 | 0 |
| Blind challenge (16, C01–C05) | 0 | 0 | 0 | **16** |

The 16 blind-challenge excerpts are **summaries written in the coder's words**, not quotations. In at least one case the summary changes the legal content:
- C01_PR04 reads "The Commission **may** seek the opinion of an approved laboratory…".
- The official text of Directive 86/361/EEC, Art 8(3), reads: "If the reasons given for the Member State's decision are as described in paragraph 1 (a), the Commission shall immediately consult the Member States concerned. If no agreement is reached without [sic] four weeks, the Commission **shall** seek the opinion of one of the approved laboratories notified in accordance with Article 7…" (quoted verbatim from `01_BASELINES/official_texts/31986L0361.txt`).

The human decision H_002 ("the Commission must seek the opinion… if consultation does not resolve the dispute") matches the official text, **not** the summary. This indicates the adjudicator went back to the legal text. The paraphrase shows a `MODALITY_LOSS` + `CONDITION_LOSS` risk in summary layers. That risk is relevant both to our pipeline and to any free-text description field (see R09 E7).
