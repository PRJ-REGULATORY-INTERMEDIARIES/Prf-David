L000137 | subsequent year. The total number of allowances in circulation in a given year shall be the cumulative number of
L000138 | allowances issued in the period since 1 January 2008, including the number issued pursuant to Article 13(2) of

L000144 | allowances issued in respect of those emissions. The first publication shall take place by 15 May 2017.
L000145 | 5. Each year, a number of allowances equal to 12 % of the total number of allowances in circulation, as set out in the
