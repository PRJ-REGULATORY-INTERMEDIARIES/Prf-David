L000856 | ANNEX IV
L000857 | PRINCIPLES FOR MONITORING AND REPORTING REFERRED TO IN ARTICLE 14(1)
L000858 | Monitoring of carbon dioxide emissions
L000859 | Emissions shall be monitored either by calculation or on the basis of measurement.
L000860 | Calculation
L000861 | Calculations of emissions shall be performed using the formula:
L000862 | Activity data × Emission factor × Oxidation factor
L000863 | Activity data (fuel used, production rate etc.) shall be monitored on the basis of supply data or measurement.
L000864 | Accepted emission factors shall be used. Activity-specific emission factors are acceptable for all fuels. Default factors are
L000865 | acceptable for all fuels except non-commercial ones (waste fuels such as tyres and industrial process gases). Seam-specific
L000866 | defaults for coal, and EU-specific or producer country-specific defaults for natural gas shall be further elaborated. IPCC
L000867 | default values are acceptable for refinery products. The emission factor for biomass shall be zero.
L000868 | If the emission factor does not take account of the fact that some of the carbon is not oxidised, then an additional oxidation factor shall be used. If activity-specific emission factors have been calculated and already take oxidation into
L000869 | account, then an oxidation factor need not be applied.
L000870 | Default oxidation factors developed pursuant to Directive 96/61/EC shall be used, unless the operator can demonstrate
L000871 | that activity-specific factors are more accurate.
L000872 | A separate calculation shall be made for each activity, installation and for each fuel.
L000873 | Measurement
L000874 | Measurement of emissions shall use standardised or accepted methods, and shall be corroborated by a supporting calculation of emissions.
L000875 | Monitoring of emissions of other greenhouse gases
L000876 | Standardised or accepted methods shall be used, developed by the Commission in collaboration with all relevant stakeholders and adopted in accordance with the procedure referred to in Article 23(2).
L000877 | Reporting of emissions
L000878 | Each operator shall include the following information in the report for an installation:
L000879 | A. Data identifying the installation, including:
L000880 | — Name of the installation;
L000881 | — Its address, including postcode and country;
L000882 | — Type and number of Annex I activities carried out in the installation;
L000883 | — Address, telephone, fax and email details for a contact person; and
L000884 | — Name of the owner of the installation, and of any parent company.
L000885 | B. For each Annex I activity carried out on the site for which emissions are calculated:
L000886 | — Activity data;
L000887 | — Emission factors;
L000888 | — Oxidation factors;
L000889 | — Total emissions; and
L000890 | — Uncertainty.
L000891 | C. For each Annex I activity carried out on the site for which emissions are measured:
L000892 | — Total emissions;
L000893 | — Information on the reliability of measurement methods; and
L000894 | — Uncertainty.
L000895 | D. For emissions from combustion, the report shall also include the oxidation factor, unless oxidation has already been
L000896 | taken into account in the development of an activity-specific emission factor.
L000897 | Member States shall take measures to coordinate reporting requirements with any existing reporting requirements in
L000898 | order to minimise the reporting burden on businesses.

L000900 | CRITERIA FOR VERIFICATION REFERRED TO IN ARTICLE 15
L000901 | General Principles
L000902 | 1. Emissions from each activity listed in Annex I shall be subject to verification.
L000903 | 2. The verification process shall include consideration of the report pursuant to Article 14(3) and of monitoring
L000904 | during the preceding year. It shall address the reliability, credibility and accuracy of monitoring systems and the
L000905 | reported data and information relating to emissions, in particular:
L000906 | (a) the reported activity data and related measurements and calculations;
L000907 | (b) the choice and the employment of emission factors;
L000908 | (c) the calculations leading to the determination of the overall emissions; and
L000909 | (d) if measurement is used, the appropriateness of the choice and the employment of measuring methods.
L000910 | 3. Reported emissions may only be validated if reliable and credible data and information allow the emissions to be
L000911 | determined with a high degree of certainty. A high degree of certainty requires the operator to show that:
L000912 | (a) the reported data is free of inconsistencies;
L000913 | (b) the collection of the data has been carried out in accordance with the applicable scientific standards; and
L000914 | (c) the relevant records of the installation are complete and consistent.
L000915 | 4. The verifier shall be given access to all sites and information in relation to the subject of the verification.
L000916 | 5. The verifier shall take into account whether the installation is registered under the Community eco-management
L000917 | and audit scheme (EMAS).
L000918 | Methodology
L000919 | Strategic analysis
L000920 | 6. The verification shall be based on a strategic analysis of all the activities carried out in the installation. This
L000921 | requires the verifier to have an overview of all the activities and their significance for emissions.
L000922 | Process analysis
L000923 | 7. The verification of the information submitted shall, where appropriate, be carried out on the site of the installation.
L000924 | The verifier shall use spot-checks to determine the reliability of the reported data and information.
L000925 | Risk analysis
L000926 | 8. The verifier shall submit all the sources of emissions in the installation to an evaluation with regard to the reliability of the data of each source contributing to the overall emissions of the installation.
L000927 | 9. On the basis of this analysis the verifier shall explicitly identify those sources with a high risk of error and other
L000928 | aspects of the monitoring and reporting procedure which are likely to contribute to errors in the determination of
L000929 | the overall emissions. This especially involves the choice of the emission factors and the calculations necessary to
L000930 | determine the level of the emissions from individual sources. Particular attention shall be given to those sources
L000931 | with a high risk of error and the abovementioned aspects of the monitoring procedure.
L000932 | 10. The verifier shall take into consideration any effective risk control methods applied by the operator with a view to
L000933 | minimising the degree of uncertainty.
L000934 | Report
L000935 | 11. The verifier shall prepare a report on the validation process stating whether the report pursuant to Article 14(3) is
L000936 | satisfactory. This report shall specify all issues relevant to the work carried out. A statement that the report
L000937 | pursuant to Article 14(3) is satisfactory may be made if, in the opinion of the verifier, the total emissions are not
L000938 | materially misstated.
L000939 | Minimum competency requirements for the verifier
L000940 | 12. The verifier shall be independent of the operator, carry out his activities in a sound and objective professional
L000941 | manner, and understand:
L000942 | (a) the provisions of this Directive, as well as relevant standards and guidance adopted by the Commission
L000943 | pursuant to Article 14(1);
L000944 | (b) the legislative, regulatory, and administrative requirements relevant to the activities being verified; and
L000945 | (c) the generation of all information related to each source of emissions in the installation, in particular, relating
