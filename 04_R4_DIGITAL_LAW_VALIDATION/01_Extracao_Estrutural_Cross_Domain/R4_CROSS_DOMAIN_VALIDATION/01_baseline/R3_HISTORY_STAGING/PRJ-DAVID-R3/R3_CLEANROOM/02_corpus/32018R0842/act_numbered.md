L000306 | and product use, agriculture and waste as determined pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 525/2013, excluding greenhouse
L000307 | gas emissions from the activities listed in Annex I to Directive 2003/87/EC.

L000309 | emissions and removals covered by Regulation (EU) 2018/841.

L000311 | treated as zero.
