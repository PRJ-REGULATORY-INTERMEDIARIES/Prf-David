# R3 Dataset Proposal for David Levi-Faur

This package presents a research dataset proposal developed from a clean-room reconstruction of five EU climate-law instruments. It is designed for conceptual and methodological discussion, not as a completed population study.

## Recommended reading order

1. `R3_EXECUTIVE_NOTE_FOR_DAVID.md` — one-page overview and five questions for discussion.
2. `R3_DATASET_PROPOSAL_MEMO_FOR_DAVID.md` — full explanation of the research question, method, current pilot, and proposal.
3. `R3_DATASET_PROPOSAL_FOR_DAVID_V1.xlsx` — the human-facing dataset proposal and empirical pilot tables.
4. `R3_DAVID_GROUPED_VIEW.xlsx` — the proposed parent–child presentation centered on LAW × INTERMEDIARY.
5. `R3_METHOD_PIPELINE_FOR_DAVID.md` — concise, auditable account of the reconstruction and validation process.
6. `R3_MECHANISM_CODEBOOK_RESEARCHER_ADJUDICATED.md` — provisional ten-mechanism vocabulary.
7. `R3_DATASET_ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL.md` — normalized analytical design.
8. `R3_DATA_DICTIONARY_DRAFT.csv`, `R3_RESEARCH_QUESTION_VARIABLE_MAP.csv`, and `R3_DECISION_PROVENANCE_EXTRACT.csv` — technical support files.

## What is authoritative

The Excel workbooks are human-facing views. The normalized, linked R3 tables remain the analytical source of truth. Summaries in the workbooks are derived from the frozen five-case pilot and should not be read as population frequencies.

## Status of the proposal

- RQ-A—how mechanisms are organized and combined—is descriptively demonstrated in the pilot.
- RQ-B—what regulatory capacities configurations may provide—remains a future theoretical and empirical layer.
- `IMPLEMENTATION` is a researcher-added provisional mechanism under decision `R3-D08-IMPLEMENTATION`; it is not attributed to David and remains `FAMILY_UNASSIGNED_PROVISIONAL`.
- No regulatory capacity or Regulatory Intermediation Architecture classification is included.
