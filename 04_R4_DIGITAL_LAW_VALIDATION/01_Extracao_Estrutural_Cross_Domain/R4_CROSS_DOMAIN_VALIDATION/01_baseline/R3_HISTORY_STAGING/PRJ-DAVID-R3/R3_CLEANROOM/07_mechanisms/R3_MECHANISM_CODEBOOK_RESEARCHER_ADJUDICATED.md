# R3 Mechanism Codebook — Researcher-Adjudicated Provisional Version

`MECHANISM_ONTOLOGY_STATUS = R3_PROVISIONAL_RESEARCHER_ADJUDICATED`

`N_MECHANISMS = 10`

This operational ontology is provisional, R3-specific, and researcher-adjudicated. It is not presented as validated, final, universal, exhaustive, or as a David Levi-Faur taxonomy. Mechanisms describe the elementary transformation performed by I within a relation that has independently passed the three-condition R–I–T gate.

## Original nine-code operational vocabulary

### ARTICULATION

#### TRANSMISSION

- **Definition:** moves information, a signal, report, obligation, or demand from one actor to another without materially transforming its content.
- **Diagnostic question:** What is being transported from A to B?
- **Include rule:** code when the intermediary's dominant contribution is passage or relay of a regulatory input or output.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude when multiple inputs are substantively combined, content changes register, meaning is clarified, or judgment is applied.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `AGGREGATION`, `TRANSLATION`, `REPRESENTATION`.

#### AGGREGATION

- **Definition:** combines multiple inputs into an organized or consolidated output.
- **Diagnostic question:** Are multiple inputs being synthesized into one organized result?
- **Include rule:** code when consolidation itself is a legally meaningful regulatory transformation.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude when combination is merely ancillary to substantive evaluation or when information is only relayed.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `TRANSMISSION`, `EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT`, `CLASSIFICATION_RANKING`.

#### REPRESENTATION

- **Definition:** articulates the interests, experiences, or preferences of a constituency in a regulatory relationship.
- **Diagnostic question:** On whose behalf does the intermediary speak?
- **Include rule:** code when constituency articulation is the intermediary's formal mediating transformation.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude consultation without a supported R–I–T relation and ordinary transmission of submissions.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `TRANSMISSION`, consultation, participation.

### INFORMATION_TREATMENT

#### TRANSLATION

- **Definition:** converts legal, scientific, technical, or regulatory content from one register or context into another usable register.
- **Diagnostic question:** Is content being converted into a differently usable form or register?
- **Include rule:** code when the dominant mediated object is content or meaning and the output remains content in an operationally different form.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude material execution, programme delivery, conformity checking, and clarification of meaning without register conversion.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `INTERPRETATION`, `IMPLEMENTATION`, `TRANSMISSION`.

#### INTERPRETATION

- **Definition:** stabilizes or clarifies the meaning of a rule, standard, or concept.
- **Diagnostic question:** What does this rule, standard, or concept mean?
- **Include rule:** code when resolving meaning is the legally relevant transformation.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude conversion into another register, application of evaluative judgment, and mere communication.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `TRANSLATION`, `EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT`.

#### EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT

- **Definition:** applies criteria, expertise, or judgment to evidence, a proposal, conduct, or claim to produce a substantive judgment about quality, adequacy, robustness, progress, significance, or merit.
- **Diagnostic question:** What substantive judgment is produced, and against what criteria or expertise?
- **Include rule:** code when judgment rather than a binary conformity check is the dominant transformation.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude pure consolidation, observation over time, predefined conformity determination, and category assignment as the dominant operation.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `VERIFICATION`, `AGGREGATION`, `CLASSIFICATION_RANKING`.

#### CLASSIFICATION_RANKING

- **Definition:** transforms complexity into a category, score, rating, status, or ordering.
- **Diagnostic question:** Does the intermediary assign a category or position?
- **Include rule:** code when assigning the category or rank is itself the distinct regulatory transformation.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude a category that is only the consequence of a prior conformity check or substantive evaluation.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT`, `VERIFICATION`, `AGGREGATION`.

### REGULATORY_RELIABILITY

#### MONITORING

- **Definition:** observes conduct, performance, or condition over time.
- **Diagnostic question:** What is happening with the target across time?
- **Include rule:** code when longitudinal or repeated observation is the dominant regulatory transformation.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude observation used merely as evidence for a predefined conformity finding or substantive assessment.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `VERIFICATION`, `EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT`, `TRANSMISSION`.

#### VERIFICATION

- **Definition:** compares an identifiable input, claim, condition, or conduct against a predefined criterion, requirement, rule, or standard and produces a conformity-related finding or consequence.
- **Diagnostic question:** Is the claimed condition or compliance supported when checked against the predefined reference point?
- **Include rule:** require an input, a predefined reference point, a check, and a conformity-related output or consequence.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude open-ended judgment of adequacy, observation without conformity determination, and independent category construction.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT`, `MONITORING`, `CLASSIFICATION_RANKING`.

## Researcher-added provisional mechanism

### FAMILY_UNASSIGNED_PROVISIONAL

#### IMPLEMENTATION

- **Definition:** a regulatory intermediary operationally enacts a regulator's legally structured programme, measure, entitlement, or regulatory intervention vis-à-vis a target through a formally assigned third-party role.
- **Typical structure:** `regulatory programme / authorized measure / resources → I → operational execution or delivery → T`.
- **Diagnostic question:** Does the intermediary convert an upstream regulatory decision, programme, or legally authorized measure into concrete regulatory action or delivery toward the target?
- **Include rule:** code only after the relation independently passes the R–I–T gate and I has a formally recognized role executing a programme, operationalizing authorized measures, administering implementation toward T, or delivering benefits, services, investments, or measures that constitute the operative R–T link.
- **Exclude rule:** exclude an actor merely complying as T; mere transmission; content conversion; conformity checking; adequacy evaluation; and commercial or logistical service without a demonstrable role in an R–T regulatory relationship.
- **Nearest boundaries:** `TRANSLATION`, ordinary policy or service delivery, direct R–T implementation, non-RIT contracting.
- **Family status:** `FAMILY_UNASSIGNED_PROVISIONAL`. No fourth family has been created.

This mechanism was added provisionally by researcher adjudication after three empirically supported RIT relations reached a codebook boundary during Phase 8B.

## Methodological provenance

The tenth mechanism emerged because three supported empirical relations could not be classified without stretching the nine-code vocabulary. Their historical sequence remains visible as Phase 8 `MECHANISM_UNRESOLVED`, Phase 8B `CODEBOOK_BOUNDARY`, researcher decision `R3-D08-IMPLEMENTATION`, and Phase 8C `IMPLEMENTATION`.

Adding `IMPLEMENTATION` does not imply that all implementation actors are regulatory intermediaries. Coding remains conditional on: (1) an identifiable R–T regulatory relationship; (2) a distinct institutionalized third-party role; and (3) a demonstrable mediating function.
