# R4.1 Method Note

R4.1 established the official digital corpus without substantive regulatory-intermediation analysis.

## Frozen source policy

The corpus contains exactly three acts: GDPR (`32016R0679`), Digital Services Act (`32022R2065`) and Artificial Intelligence Act (`32024R1689`). For each, the original Official Journal act as published was selected. The fixed EUR-Lex ELI URL is recorded as the canonical reference, while the official CELLAR CELEX XHTML representation supplied the preserved machine-readable source.

## Source and processing separation

Original XHTML files are preserved unedited in `02_sources/<CASE_ID>/original/`. UTF-8 processing copies are stored separately in `normalized/`. Processing copies contain only mechanical text extraction, whitespace normalization and explicit structural delimiters; they do not paraphrase, translate, summarize, reorder or semantically compress the text.

## Validation result

All three acts passed structural validation. The indexes record 99, 93 and 113 articles respectively; 173, 156 and 180 recitals; and 0, 0 and 13 annex containers. Article and recital sequences were coherent, document endings were present, and the AI Act annexes were preserved.

The overall status is `VALIDATED_WITH_NOTES` because human acceptance of the selected source/version remains required by the governing protocol. This is a review status, not an unresolved source failure.

## Methodological boundary

No actor names were extracted for analytical purposes, no R–I–T relationships were reconstructed, and no mechanism keywords were searched. Rotem Medzini’s coding was not consulted. The R3 baseline and codebook remain unchanged.

## Model transparency

The protocol designates Luna High for this mechanical phase and Terra High only for substantive ambiguity. The Codex runtime used here did not expose a selectable Luna/Terra variant; logs therefore identify the runtime limitation instead of falsely assigning a model. No escalation occurred.
