# RGAM — PAIRED DECISION RULES v1.0 (frozen before case execution)

**Companion to** `RGAM_PAIRED_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL_v1_0.md`. Each rule cites its **controlling basis**: `MS` = manuscript v0.6.1 (Level 1), `ON` = ontology v0.4 rev3 (Level 2), `PR` = precedence/version records (Level 3), `TPL` = template rules v1.2 (Level 4, procedural only), `WRAP` = execution-wrapper decision where the sources are silent (registered in Appendix A). **Change policy:** none after freeze; deviations are logged by the executor in PROTOCOL_DEVIATION_LOG, never applied silently.

## A. Authority, independence, corpus

**RGAM-DR-01 — Source hierarchy.** MS prevails over ON where they conflict materially; PR documents establish precedence and unresolved conflicts only; TPL is used only as a procedural template where consistent with MS. Conflicts are recorded in the conflict register and are never silently harmonized. *(User directive; PR: alignment §precedence)*

**RGAM-DR-02 — No import.** No operation list from the earlier experimental procedure, no V8 vocabulary or rule, no content of `RGAM/V8_ACTIVE/`, no historical case result, and no drafting-prompt content is a method input. If an RGAM rule were found to exist only in `V8_ACTIVE/` it would be recorded `RGAM_PROTOCOL_DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED` and not imported; none was found. *(User directive)*

**RGAM-DR-03 — Corpus W.** W is the complete frozen official source file of the case: fixed version (original act as published, no consolidation), fixed temporal reference, jurisdiction, domain stated from the instrument's own title/object clause, inclusion rule (all units of the file), declared exclusions, source provenance and SHA-256. The run **stops** if the verified hash differs from the capsule's expected hash or if the structural parse is inconsistent with the neutral metadata without a described reason. `W ≠ A`. *(MS §2.4, §8.4(1); ON §5.1)*

**RGAM-DR-04 — Temporal reference and legal status.** The temporal reference is declared in W_DECLARATION from the act's own entry-into-force, application and (where the neutral metadata states it) repeal-related information; legal status of each norm is recorded for that reference as one of `in_force`, `not_yet_applicable`, `suspended`, `expired_or_repealed`, `conditional_on_further_act`, with a validity note. The baseline is **not** asserted to be the law applicable at any other date. *(MS §4.4, §8.4(1); TPL §2 generalized)*

## B. Units, coverage, statements

**RGAM-DR-05 — Units and identifiers.** Unit = article paragraph; points `…pt{x}`; single-paragraph article `P0`; annex point `ANX{n}.pt{x}`; recital `REC{n}`. Record ids: `ST-`, `NA-`, `PD-`, `QR-`, `EXT-`, `SC-`, `ARCH-`, `PROP-`, `CL-`, `UNC-`, `HQ-`, `LNK-`, `TR-`, `DEV-`, `RUN-`. (The template's `AI-nn` prefix for architectural inference is not used to avoid collision with an instrument name; architectural assessment lives in ARCH records.) Provenance is many-to-many: a record lists all supporting units and each link is mirrored in PROVENANCE_LINKS. *(TPL §1; WRAP-ID)*

**RGAM-DR-06 — Full coverage.** Every unit of W is inventoried and screened in source order. `governance_relevance` is Yes (at least one derived statement/record), No (none) or Uncertain. A "No" is legitimate. Editorial headings, titles and chapters are never evidence of architectural membership. Coverage must be auditable: units screened = units inventoried. *(TPL §3; MS §8.4(2), §2.4)*

**RGAM-DR-07 — Recitals.** Interpretive context only. No assignment, position, device, relation or external reference is created from a recital alone; a recital may be cited in an evidence note. Recitals are inventoried and screened. *(TPL §11; MS §8.4 last paragraph)*

**RGAM-DR-08 — Annexes and other formal provisions.** Annexes and formal provisions that are part of the enacted text are units of W and are screened and coded like operative provisions; content that cannot be represented in the source file (e.g., table geometry) is declared in W_DECLARATION.exclusions and in the uncertainty log. Provisions that amend another instrument are recorded as EXT (`reference_role` = other, described) and not as architectural relations of W. *(GAP-01, GAP-02; MS §8.4(2) "every operative provision")*

**RGAM-DR-09 — Institutional statements.** From each provision with governance relevance the executor derives zero or more minimal institutional statements (E), each citing ≥1 unit and a verbatim span (≤400 characters). Every NA, PD, QR and EXT cites ≥1 statement. A statement adds no content absent from the unit. *(MS §6.2 (E), §7.1)*

## C. Parts

**RGAM-DR-10 — Normative assignments.** An NA attaches a duty, power or permission to a position when no counterparty is identifiable; it carries function, mechanism and instrument as separate slots, modality, conditions, norm type and the constitution it effects and does **not** effect. **A counterparty is never invented to complete a binary representation.** Where a provision identifies a recipient, forum or counterparty the statement yields a QR, not an NA duplicate; one provision may yield both an NA and a QR only where they express different statements. *(MS §6.2; TPL §4.1, §20.v1.1; ON §3.3 counterexample)*

**RGAM-DR-11 — Positions.** A position is a legally specified place bearing at least one NA or QR: an organization, an office or an actor category. An office is not its holder. Position model: TYPE (generic legal category) or INSTANCE (specific institution, recorded only with its own source and never inferred from a type); jurisdiction; temporal reference; stated legal multiplicity (PT-7). Excluded: a source document; a named holder; a mention without any NA/QR (e.g., a recital-only mention); a platform or instrument below the device threshold. Role is relation-relative; the same position may bear different roles in different records. *(MS §6.2; ON §3.1 with MS precedence, conflict C-28; ON D08, PT-7)*

**RGAM-DR-12 — Instruments and devices.** Four kinds are kept apart: position; instrument (typed category of tool, recorded as INS-TYPE characteristic of an NA/QR); institutional device; documentary artifact. A **device (DEV)** requires (a) identity — it can be singled out — and (b) an operator, or governing rules, or a legally specified bounded function. Below the threshold: INS-TYPE. Borderline: INS-TYPE plus queue (HQ-DEV). A device is never an actor/position; an artifact (e.g., a certificate) is an output, not a device; a visualization with no legal effect is neither. Promotion INS-TYPE→DEV needs a recorded source-based reason. *(MS §6.2, §6.3; ON §3.2; TPL §9)*

**RGAM-DR-13 — Norm types.** Classify every establishment-related provision by wording on the authentic text: N1 direct establishment; N2 obligation to establish; N3 permission or competence; N4 condition on legal effect (compound and ambiguous wording recorded as such and queued, HQ-NORMTYPE). **An entity is `constituted_by_N1` only under N1 (or an N1 element of a compound).** N2 constitutes the obligation (and a relation if a counterparty is identified) but not the entity; N3 a competence; N4 a conditional constitution effective after the condition. Where an instrument obliges a body (e.g., a state) to provide for or designate an institution, the institution is a type-level position referenced by the instrument and **no instance is created**. Drafting cues in translated text are not inferred. *(MS §4.3; TPL §8; conflict C-02)*

## D. Relations

**RGAM-DR-14 — Admission of a QR.** Both parties are identifiable in the same provision or by explicit cross-reference (a defined category or an institutional forum may be a party); modality is not prohibition; the evidence is source-grounded. Excluded: relations inferred from the presence of two actors, from a network drawing or from an obligation on one party alone; relations resting on a recital alone. *(MS §6.2; ON §3.3; TPL §4.3)*

**RGAM-DR-15 — Relation fields and vocabulary.** Relation type: the ten RGAM relation types recorded in ON §3.3 (authority/control, delegation/authorization, oversight/supervision, accountability, information/reporting, verification/assurance, review/adjudication, representation/participation, contractual/exchange, beneficiary/protective); where a provision supports two types, two QR records are made; content fitting none is coded to the nearest type with `type_note` and a flag (no new type is created). Function vocabulary and modality vocabulary as in the data dictionary (TPL-derived; GAP-05). **Direction convention:** `origin` is the legal subject of the statement (obligor/power-holder), `counterpart` the right-holder, addressee or recipient; rights stated from the right-holder's side are recorded with modality `entitlement` and the obligor as origin; where governance direction differs, `governance_direction` is also recorded. Function, mechanism and instrument remain three separate slots (mechanism is a means of governing, not a generative mechanism). *(ON §3.3; TPL §5–7, §20 v1.1–1.2; MS §6.2, §7.1)*

**RGAM-DR-16 — Prohibitions.** A prohibition is a norm about an action or relation, **not** a connection that exists. It is recorded as an NA with `modality = prohibition` and `constraint_only = Y` (with the counterpart field filled if identifiable) and is **not** admitted to R. "Not found" is a statement about the search; silence is neither permission nor prohibition. *(MS §6.2; PR: alignment #2; conflict C-08)*

**RGAM-DR-17 — Accountability.** Three substantive types plus a separate evidential assessment. INFO: reporting, disclosure or publicity without duty of justification (never accountability on its own). ANSWERABILITY: identifiable actor–forum relation with a duty to explain/justify and institutionalized questioning capacity, without assessment/judgment capacity or a grounded possibility of consequences (flag `answerability_only`, list missing elements). ACCOUNTABILITY: all of these plus institutionalized assessment/judgment capacity and a possibility of consequences grounded in the legal source. Sufficiency: `sufficient` or `indeterminate` (missing elements recorded); an assertion of accountability with no identifiable forum, duty or channel is not a fourth type but insufficient evidence for any type. Distributed or public forums are admissible where their capacities are institutionally identifiable. Supervision alone is not accountability though it may supply assessment capacity where a duty to explain is also present (then `derived`). Exercise is not inferred. One accountability relation exists as a single QR object; other relations or arrangements carry `accountable_via` pointers; no duplicate observation. *(MS §6.4; ON §3.3.1 (D02, D04, D10) with MS precedence, conflict C-09; TPL §10)*

## E. Statuses and materiality

**RGAM-DR-18 — Two axes.** Every NA, PD and QR carries `legal_status_of_norm` and `epistemic_status`; modality, condition, legal status and derivation are separate fields. Epistemic statuses: `conceptually_projected`, `documented`, `legal_constitution_evidenced`, `material_establishment_evidenced`, `exercise_evidenced` — attached to individual claims, each needing its own evidence, none inferred from another, not a life cycle. From legal text only: `documented` (provision exists in the frozen source) and `legal_constitution_evidenced` (the instrument's own provisions evidence what the norm constitutes and its force/applicability for the temporal reference). `material_establishment_evidenced` applies to positions and devices only (PT-2) and is **not collected** (`not_assessed`); `exercise_evidenced` is not collected. A relation never carries `material_establishment_evidenced`. *(MS §4.4; ON §4 PT-2; conflict C-01, C-03)*

**RGAM-DR-19 — Materiality as transversal control.** Materiality attaches to claims; it is not a phase. Retention rule: a claim is kept at the highest status its evidence supports; a claim with no nameable material referent at the asserted status is down-coded and flagged and may not be presented as "reconstructed" at the higher status; nothing is deleted. Relations carry `depends_on` and a support-gap report; dependencies are expectations, never gates. An accessible source file shows documentation only. A mandated-but-unestablished entity is documented; what is constituted is the obligation (N2). Customary constitution is out of scope (PT-3), flagged not denied. *(MS §4.4–4.5; ON §4 PT-2, PT-3; WRAP none)*

## F. References, paths, closure

**RGAM-DR-20 — External references and cross-references.** An EXT record is made for every reference to a source outside W (another instrument, standard, administrative act, other normative document): `external_reference_id`, referenced source, `source_kind`, `reference_role`, `source_verification_status`, `boundary_status`, `constitutive_dependency`. A reference shows legal relevance only. **No external retrieval is authorized; every EXT stays `not_checked` and `outside_corpus_not_expanded`**; relations in the referenced source never enter an architecture. A reference that merely identifies a referent already defined in W (an intra-instrument cross-reference) resolves within W and is recorded as supporting units/derivation, not EXT (queue HQ-XREF if ambiguous). A reference introduces new substantive evidence only if the referenced content is itself in W. An element whose legal constitution depends on an unresolved external reference is `undetermined`. *(MS §6.5, §8.4(6); ON §3.7 (D05, PT-6, PT-8); TPL §4.4; GAP-03, GAP-04; conflict C-12)*

**RGAM-DR-21 — Reachability is not transmission.** No forwarding, data flow or operative dependency is inferred from graph paths. A path property is a candidate positional property until a legal rule links the relations; path semantics (which relation types, modalities and conditions constitute a step) must be stated for every path-based property. *(MS §6.7; TPL §13)*

**RGAM-DR-22 — Absence and closure.** Absence-dependent properties (necessity of a stage, substitutability, exclusivity, "no alternative path", absence of a relation) are asserted only with a declared coverage/closure statement (provisions and instruments searched, and how); otherwise `undetermined`. An unresolved external reference with constitutive dependency touching the path makes the property `undetermined` or "within the declared corpus only". Absence of an edge never proves separation. *(MS §4.7, §6.2 I4; ON PT-4, PT-8; TPL §16)*

## G. Configuration and architecture

**RGAM-DR-23 — Subconfigurations.** See Protocol §7. A candidate S is defined from relations, never from chapters or vocabulary; it records pattern rule, governance question, constituents, organizing mechanism, warrant basis, boundary, overlap, nesting, cross-part span, uncertainty and a competing grouping. Alexander's closure axiom is **not** imported: the intersection of two S is not automatically an independently constituted third. A single reporting relation is not a reporting system where nothing receives, assesses or follows up. *(MS §2.2, §6.2; ON §3.4; TPL §14)*

**RGAM-DR-24 — Properties, bearers, scale, aggregation, part–whole.** See Protocol §8. Four kinds and bearers; ten architectural dimensions of ON §6.1 in five families F1–F5 (organizing proposal; not validated metrics); no dimension reduced to a score; discretion kept by function × position × qualified relation; values per S reported before any summary; atomistic, ecological and premature-aggregation checks recorded; an instrument "strongly intermediated in one function and centralized in another" is not "an intermediated statute". Graph or spatial indices (including centrality as a number) are **not** computed (MS §5.3). *(MS §5.3, §6.6; ON §6, §6.1; conflict C-11, C-24)*

**RGAM-DR-25 — S→A (I1–I4).** See Protocol §9. Outcomes `INTEGRATED`, `DISTINGUISHABLE`, `OVERLAPPING_OR_PARTIALLY_INTEGRATED`, `INDETERMINATE`; a "distinct architectures" outcome requires sufficient declared coverage; unresolved coverage or constitutive external reference → `INDETERMINATE`. Each candidate answers I1–I4 in the anti-circularity sequence; each outcome states its basis and the rival grouping. The instrument-level result is a profile: `NO_ARCHITECTURE_WARRANTED`, `ONE_ARCHITECTURE`, `SEVERAL_DISTINGUISHABLE`, `OVERLAPPING_OR_PARTIALLY_INTEGRATED` or `INDETERMINATE`. Token note: the manuscript's single term "undetermined" is represented as `undetermined` for properties/absence claims and `INDETERMINATE` for S→A outcomes (same meaning; conflict C-10). *(MS §6.2; TPL §15; ON §3.5)*

**RGAM-DR-26 — Instrument-level profile.** The result for the instrument is a multidimensional profile (properties per S and per A, each with scale, bearer and aggregation) plus a status profile (descriptive tallies of claim statuses, e.g., "N relations `legal_constitution_evidenced`; 0 `material_establishment_evidenced`; 0 `exercise_evidenced`"). It is never one label or one score. *(MS §6.2, §4.7; ON §3.5, §4)*

## H. Stopping, review, traceability, layers

**RGAM-DR-27 — Stopping rule.** Assert only the highest analytical level authorized by the evidence; stop at E, P, R, S or A where the evidence stops; `INDETERMINATE` and `NO_ARCHITECTURE_WARRANTED` are legitimate; `W → A` is never forced. *(MS §4.7; ON §5.5; TPL §15, §16)*

**RGAM-DR-28 — Machine status and human review.** All records are machine-generated and provisional; repetition is not reliability; consequential ambiguity goes to the human queue (HQ), never silently resolved; the executor never fills `human_*` columns and never simulates adjudication. Mandatory triggers: HQ-NORMTYPE, HQ-DEV, HQ-ACCOUNT, HQ-INTEGRATION (every INTEGRATED), HQ-OVERLAP (every OVERLAPPING), HQ-XREF, HQ-DIRECTION, HQ-GAP, HQ-EXTDEP. *(TPL §18; ON §10; PR: alignment "human adjudication")*

**RGAM-DR-29 — Traceability.** Vertical trace `A → S → R/NA/PD → ST → unit → verbatim span` must resolve for every record at every level; IDs stable; inference rules, epistemic status, bearer and uncertainty flags present. Trace demonstrations: after screening and construction of the candidate S/A list, choose (i) one candidate by seeded pseudo-random draw among candidates citing units from ≥2 distinct parts of the instrument, over the list sorted by id, seed = integer value of the first 8 hexadecimal digits of the case source SHA-256; (ii) the candidate with the largest number of distinct supporting units. Neither is chosen for expected support. *(MS §6.2, §7.7; TPL §17 generalized — instrument-specific seed removed)*

**RGAM-DR-30 — Layer discipline.** D (strategies, logics) and X (implementation, operation, outcomes, enactment) are not coded beyond a note where the text itself states an implementation requirement; observed arrangements are not R; research views are representations, not architectures; ISO-inspired correspondences link representations and are not legal relations. *(MS §6.2, §7.7; ON §3.8; TPL §19)*

## I. Experiment governance

**RGAM-DR-31 — Case independence.** One case per fresh sequential subcontext; no content of another case, of any V8 output or of any historical extraction; sequence fixed by the capsule order in the capsule register. The orchestrator does not paste one case's results into another case's brief. *(User directive; Blindness policy)*

**RGAM-DR-32 — Deviations and gaps.** Any deviation or encountered gap is logged in PROTOCOL_DEVIATION_LOG with the rule id and is queued (HQ-GAP). The method is not adapted to a case during execution.

**RGAM-DR-33 — V8–RGAM crosswalk placeholder.** `PLACEHOLDER — a V8–RGAM crosswalk will be produced after both arms are frozen.` It is not constructed, anticipated or approximated here, and no RGAM term is renamed to match another vocabulary.
