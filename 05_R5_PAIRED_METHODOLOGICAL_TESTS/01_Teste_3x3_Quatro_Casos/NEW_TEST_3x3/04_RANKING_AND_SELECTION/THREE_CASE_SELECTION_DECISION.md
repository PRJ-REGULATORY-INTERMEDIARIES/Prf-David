## 4. Why a naive global ranking would have been misleading (illustration, not used)

If raw numbers from every family were sorted together, the first place would go to the .eu Regulation 874/2004 (6 "derived R–I–T candidates"), followed by Social Climate Fund and CBAM (5 each) — but 874/2004's figure is six model-pass edges over six purposively selected provisions, pre-adjudication, produced because Rotem had coded intermediaries there. This is exactly the incompatibility the comparability gate exists to prevent. Likewise the GDPR/DSA/AI Act actor counts (195/139/179 textual candidates) are not comparable with R3 actor counts (8–34).
