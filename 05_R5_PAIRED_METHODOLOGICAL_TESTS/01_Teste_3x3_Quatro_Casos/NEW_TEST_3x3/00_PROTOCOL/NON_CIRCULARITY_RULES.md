# NEW TEST 3x3 — NON-CIRCULARITY RULES

Selection must be independent of the outcome to be tested later (V8 architectural reconstruction).

## 1. Forbidden as selection inputs

- architecture previously found; architectural coherence;
- centrality, modularity, hierarchy;
- network density calculated for this new test;
- existence of attractive governance patterns; theoretical usefulness;
- agreement with David's argument; agreement with Igor's expectations;
- performance of V8 in previous runs (including V8_COMP_01);
- performance of the eight-operation protocol;
- Codex/Claude agreement;
- any later analytical result.

| Path (read-only, previous experiments) | Why prohibited |
|---|---|
| `COMPARATIVE_PILOTS/V8_COMP_01_2026-10-04/TRACK_A/`, `TRACK_B/`, `CROSS_MODEL_REPLICATION/` | V8 / V8+8-operations outputs |

These paths are listed in the case universe only as **existing, non-used** artifacts. No metric was taken from them.

## 3. Material permitted (case identification and observational counts only)

Only pre-existing extraction records that count entities, not architectures: actor registers, relation tables, intermediary registers, corpus/source registers, codebooks, and provenance files. Counts are used only as defined in `METRIC_DEFINITION_REGISTER.md`.

## 4. Pointer-only reads (disclosed)

The following files from the V8_COMP_01 stream were read **only as an index to locate cases and sources**, and nothing in them influenced inclusion, exclusion, comparability or rank:

- `EXISTING_LEGAL_CORPUS_INVENTORY.csv`, `CASE_SELECTION_MEMO.md`, `CASE_SCOPE_AND_SELECTION_REGISTER.md`, `01_MDR/MDR_SOURCE_CONTROL.md`, `02_DSA/README.md`, `03_LEI_13848/README.md`.

Their "readiness" / "best candidate" judgments were disregarded. V8_COMP_01's two-case choice (91/263/EEC and 874/2004) has no role in New Test 3x3.

## 5. Quantities that are *not* architectural results and may be used

Counts of actors, regulators, intermediaries, targets, relations and intermediation relations as recorded in pre-existing extraction records, subject to the comparability gate. Mechanism, configuration and accountability fields from earlier datasets are not used.

## 6. Self-audit questions applied to every decision

1. Would this decision have been the same if V8 had never been run?
2. Is it justified by scope, provenance, definition or subject matter?
3. Does it avoid any value judgement about which governance pattern is attractive?

Decisions failing any question are reversed and logged.
