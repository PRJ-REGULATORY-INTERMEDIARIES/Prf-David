# Architecture register

## A01 — Fund-specific formal public funding architecture

- **Property bearer:** The formal legal design constituted by Regulation (EU) 2023/955 as a whole within the stated boundary, not the Commission–Member State dyad and not every component relationship.
- **Claim:** The Regulation establishes a Commission-managed, national-plan-mediated, performance-conditioned and control-intensive public funding architecture with vertically distributed implementation.
- **Supporting configurations:** CFG01 (Plan formation and authorization), CFG02 (direct-managed performance-conditioned financing), and CFG03 (national implementation and financial accountability).
- **Evidence:** Articles 1, 4–7, 12, 16–21 and 24–27; Annex III points 1–3. The exact supporting relations and traceability links are in the registers and matrix.
- **Inferential step:** The three configurations are linked in the legal sequence from eligibility and Plan design, through Commission approval and agreement, to implementation, performance review, financial control and evaluation. Commission decisions occupy legally consequential allocation/payment stages; Member States retain the implementation and national control functions. This combination warrants the bounded architectural characterization.
- **Architectural significance:** Significance rests on the mandatory Plan gateway, Commission decision authority, payment sequence and legally consequential suspension/reduction/termination/recovery powers, together with the placement of national implementation and controls. It is not inferred from how often actors or provisions appear.
- **Confidence/status:** Medium-high; inferential architecture claim, not directly stated as a named architecture in the Regulation.
- **Limits:** Formal design only. The architecture claim covers the Fund-specific legal arrangement in this instrument, not the broader climate, energy, cohesion or social-protection regimes; it does not establish implementation, effectiveness, actual centralization of every function, polycentricity, self-regulation, or intermediary-centered governance. Article 28's interface with Regulation (EU) 2021/1060 is not enough to reconstruct the architecture of that separate framework.
