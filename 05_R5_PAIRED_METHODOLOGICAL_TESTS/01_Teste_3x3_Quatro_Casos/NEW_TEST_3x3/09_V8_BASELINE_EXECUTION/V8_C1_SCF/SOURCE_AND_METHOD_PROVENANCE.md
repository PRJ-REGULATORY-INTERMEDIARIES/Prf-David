## Method application

The analysis preserves V8's levels: Level I elements (objects, actors, functions, mechanisms, instruments, relationships); Level II relational attributes (authority, discretion, accountability); and Level III strategies, configurations, architectures, regimes and governance logics. Relations are the bridge from evidence to configurations. Higher-order claims are bounded to the legal design constituted by this Regulation and remain vertically traceable. Architectural significance is assessed from authority, sequence, consequence, structural position and substitutability, not provision frequency. Formal legal design is not treated as evidence of implementation or operation. A configuration or architecture is not attributed to a dyad: the principal property bearer is the Fund-specific legal design in Regulation (EU) 2023/955.

The execution uses the manuscript's relational/configurational method and the active V8 baseline/codebook. It does not import any separate draft's operation checklist as a V8 requirement.
