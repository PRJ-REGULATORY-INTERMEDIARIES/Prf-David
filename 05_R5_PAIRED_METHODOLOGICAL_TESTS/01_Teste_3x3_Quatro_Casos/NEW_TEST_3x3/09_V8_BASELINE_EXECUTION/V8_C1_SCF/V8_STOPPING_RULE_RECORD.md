# Stopping-rule record

## Highest level supported

The available source supports a bounded Level III claim about the **formal Fund-specific governance architecture constituted by Regulation (EU) 2023/955**. The evidence supports three configurations: Plan formation/authorization, direct-managed performance-conditioned financing, and national implementation/financial accountability. Their sequencing and legally consequential decision and payment powers support the qualified architecture description in A01.

## Why analysis stops here

The analysis stops before a whole-regulatory-regime claim. Regime boundaries and relations require evidence across other laws, institutions, standards, and practices. Cross-references and the amendment in Article 28 identify interfaces, but do not establish the organization of those wider systems. The analysis also stops before any implementation or operational claim: the frozen text states legal duties, powers and procedures, not whether they were exercised or effective.

No inference is made that the arrangement is polycentric, self-regulatory, or intermediary-centered. Multiple actors alone do not establish polycentricity; Article 9's pass-through channel is optional and lacks evidence of structural indispensability; and the legal text does not establish substantial autonomous self-regulatory capacity by regulated actors.

## Traceability and uncertainty

A01 links to CFG01–CFG03, their typed relationships R01–R09, claim IDs C03–C14, and exact article/annex anchors in `V8_TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.csv`. Confidence is medium-high because the relational and sequential design is explicit, while “control-intensive” and the architecture-level aggregation remain analytical characterizations. Uncertainties about broader regime boundaries, implementation, discretion in use, and optional delivery channels are recorded in `V8_UNCERTAINTY_REGISTER.csv`.

The stopping rule is therefore satisfied: stop at the highest level presently warranted by the complete frozen legal instrument—one bounded formal architecture claim—and do not force a regime or operational classification.
