# V8 four-case baseline — descriptive summary

## Scope

This is a descriptive summary of four separately conducted V8-only analyses, completed sequentially in the frozen order. It does not compare V8 with RGAM, evaluate methodological superiority, use historical intermediation counts as analytical evidence, or infer a common architecture across the four instruments.

## Case-level results

| Case | Claims | Typed relations | Configurations | Bounded formal architecture conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Social Climate Fund | 15 (Level I: 4; II: 6; III: 5) | 9 | 3 | Fund-specific public-funding arrangement with Commission management, national plans, performance-conditioned financing and vertically distributed implementation. |
| CBAM | 16 (Level I: 12; II: 3; III: 1) | 10 | 5 | Formal arrangement connects authorization/declaration, emissions assurance, registry, certificate obligations, review and enforcement. |
| AI Act | 14 (Level I only: 6; I/II: 2; II only: 4; III: 2) | 11 | 3 | Differentiated, hybrid and multilevel formal legal structure, including distinct high-risk-AI and GPAI pathways. |
| Directive 86/361/EEC | 21 register records (18 core I/II claims plus K01/A01/X01 at Level III) | 12 | 1 | Distributed conformity assurance under coordinated technical specification and national approval, with a Commission-level safeguard. |

Across the four individual registers there are **66 claim-register records, 42 typed relation records, 12 configurations and four bounded architecture claims**. The Directive register contains three additional Level III records (a configuration, architecture and deferred higher-order boundary record), hence its 21 rows include 18 core Level I/II claims. These are record totals, not scores or a case ranking. Each architecture claim is instrument-bounded and formal-legal only. None is a claim about implementation, operational performance, effectiveness, or the full boundaries of a broader regulatory regime.

The uncertainty registers contain 12, 11, 9 and 12 entries respectively (44 total). This includes category-coverage and screened/not-material records added during final QA; it is not a count of 44 unresolved uncertainties. Each case register explicitly covers source/reference, actor, action, counterparty, modality, condition, cross-provision, architectural-inference and stopping-rule uncertainty. Open issues and screened issues remain separately described in those registers.

## Inference limits

V8 permits a bounded formal architecture inference in each case, but the property bearer, configuration, evidence and stopping boundary differ by instrument. The four results do not warrant a single shared architecture, an overall architecture type, or conclusions about relative complexity. The case files preserve the evidence and inferential path for each claim separately.
