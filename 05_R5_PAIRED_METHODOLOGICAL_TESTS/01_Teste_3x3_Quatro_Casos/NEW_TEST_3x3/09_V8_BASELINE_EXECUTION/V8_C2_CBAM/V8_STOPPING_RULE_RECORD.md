# Stopping rule record — V8 C2 CBAM

**Highest warranted level:** formal governance architecture of CBAM under this Regulation (A1), supported by configurations CFG1–CFG5.

The analysis moves from provisions to observable elements and assignments (Level I), to typed governance relationships and relational authority, discretion and accountability attributes (Level II), then to configurations around linked governance functions (Level III). Cross-reference and functional coupling across authorization, declarations, verification, registry, certificates and review/enforcement make an architecture-level inference warranted. The inference is not based on frequency; each configuration has traceable legal anchors and a specified organizational role.

Stop at the architecture claim. Do not infer a complete regulatory regime, architecture among regimes, dominant governance logic, categorical architecture type, implementation architecture, operational architecture, or effectiveness. Although Article 1 expressly says CBAM complements the EU ETS, the source corpus does not supply the wider legal and institutional material needed to map the regime boundary or architecture-to-architecture relation. The formal provisions cannot establish actual implementation or relative operational centrality. These are evidentiary limits, not claims that such arrangements do not exist.

The property bearer for A1 is the functionally linked set of legally constituted arrangements under the Regulation. Where provisions only assign a task to an institution without specifying a counterpart, the record preserves the assignment rather than fabricating a dyadic edge. Confidence is medium-high for A1's formal existence and structure, and intentionally lower for any claim about practical significance.
