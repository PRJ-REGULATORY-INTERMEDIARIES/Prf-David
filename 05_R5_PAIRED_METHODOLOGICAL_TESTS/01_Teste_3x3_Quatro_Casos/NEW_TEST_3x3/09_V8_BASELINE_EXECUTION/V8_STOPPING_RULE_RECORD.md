# Stopping rule record

## Highest supported level

The analysis stops at **Level III: formal governance architecture** for the governance arrangements constituted by Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Configurations K01–K03 have linked legal elements, typed relationships and relational attributes traceable to the source, supporting bounded claim A01.

## Why the analysis stops here

The source supports more than isolated elements: it allocates connected functions and authority across lifecycle operator duties, conditional conformity assessment, Member State surveillance, Union safeguard review and a distinct Commission-centered GPAI pathway. Those linked configurations justify the architecture inference. The source does not establish the broader institutional boundaries and continuity needed to classify a regulatory regime, nor does it warrant a single governance logic for every pathway.

No claim is made about implementation, operation, compliance, effectiveness, actual centrality or prevalence. The architecture descriptor is based on configurational differentiation and legally consequential allocation, not frequency. Provisions that assign powers or duties without an evidenced counterparty remain assignments rather than forced actor dyads.

## Result

- Level I: coded and traceable.
- Level II: coded where authority, discretion or accountability is text-supported.
- Level III configurations: K01–K03 retained.
- Level III architecture: A01 retained, bounded to formal legal design.
- Regime and unified governance-logic inference: stopped/not claimed.
