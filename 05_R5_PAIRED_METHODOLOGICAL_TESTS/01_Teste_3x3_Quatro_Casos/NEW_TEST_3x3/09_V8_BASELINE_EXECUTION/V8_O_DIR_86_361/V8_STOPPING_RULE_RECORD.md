# Stopping rule record

The analysis stops at a bounded formal legal architecture claim (A01), based on configuration K01. This is the highest level supported by the available legal evidence under the V8 ladder.

The record supports Level I elements and typed relations; Level II authority, conditional discretion, and limited information/reason-giving attributes; and a Level III configuration joining technical specification, testing/certification, recognition, national approval, and safeguards. The architecture inference is warranted because the configuration is functionally connected and consequential: recognized certificates remove further testing in specified cases, while testing, approval, recognition, and corrective powers are allocated across identified actors and procedures.

The inference stops before broader governance logics, regime claims, or a whole-sector architecture. The Directive alone does not establish implementation, actual operation, effectiveness, the realized significance or substitutability of laboratories, or the boundaries and completeness of the broader telecommunications regulatory system. No higher-order label is assigned merely because the instrument has several actors or multiple provisions. No frequency score is used. Formal legal design is the evidentiary ceiling here.
