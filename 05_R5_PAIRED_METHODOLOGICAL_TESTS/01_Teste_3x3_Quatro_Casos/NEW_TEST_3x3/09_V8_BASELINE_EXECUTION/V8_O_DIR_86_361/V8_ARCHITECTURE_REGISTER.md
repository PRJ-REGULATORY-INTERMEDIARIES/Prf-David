# Architecture register

## A01 — bounded formal legal architecture

**Claim.** Directive 86/361/EEC constitutes a formal legal architecture for conformity assurance and mutual recognition in the covered process: distributed testing and certification feed national type approval, while coordinated specification work and a public safeguard structure the process.

**Bearer and boundary.** The bearer is the legal arrangement constituted by Directive 86/361/EEC for test results concerning mass-produced telecommunications terminal equipment. This does not characterize the whole telecommunications sector or an external regulatory regime.

**Vertical trace.** K01 is grounded in R01–R06 and R08–R12, anchored principally in Articles 4–8. The architecture-level property is the distribution and coupling of capacities: CEPT drafts technical specifications at Commission request; approved laboratories test and certify; certificate recognition removes repeat tests in specified cases; competent Member State authorities retain type approval and safeguard authority; and the Commission decides whether to withdraw a specification through the Article 8(4) sequence.

**Architectural significance.** The inference rests on legal consequence, sequence, and allocation of decision capacity. Article 6(2) makes the recognized result consequential to approval by barring further tests in the defined case. Articles 7–8 connect the evidence-producing laboratory, national approval authorities, Commission, and Committee through approval, information, suspension, opinion, withdrawal, and revocation procedures. Significance is therefore configurational; it is not inferred from actor or provision frequency.

**Classification and confidence.** Higher-order, derived from a supported configuration; confidence high for formal design. Descriptive shorthand: *distributed conformity assurance under coordinated technical specification and national approval, with a Commission-level safeguard*. This phrase describes the evidenced configuration and is not a claim that one governance type dominates all elements.

**Exclusions.** No inference is made about implementation or operational architecture, effectiveness, actual laboratory independence or competence, actual use of waivers, sector-wide intermediation centrality, a complete regulatory regime, or a comprehensive governance logic. These are unsupported by the frozen Directive corpus alone.
