# Architecture register

## A01 — differentiated, hybrid, multilevel formal governance architecture

- **Bearer:** the formal legal design constituted by Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, within its own scope.
- **Level:** III — governance architecture.
- **Status/confidence:** higher-order; high confidence, bounded to formal design.
- **Supporting configurations:** K01 high-risk system lifecycle assurance; K02 national market surveillance with Union safeguard and rights-authority links; K03 Commission-centered GPAI supervision and enforcement.
- **Traceability:** C03–C12; typed relations R01–R11; legal anchors listed in K01–K03 and the claim register.
- **Reasoning:** The Regulation links distinct actors, objects and governance functions in more than one pathway. High-risk systems combine operator-side lifecycle obligations, deployer duties, conditional notified-body assessment and public surveillance. A separate Chapter V pathway assigns exclusive Commission supervision/enforcement for GPAI models, implemented through the AI Office and connected to specified scientific-panel and Board roles. National market-surveillance powers and Union safeguard review create a further multilevel configuration. Taken together these relations support the descriptors *differentiated*, *hybrid* and *multilevel*. The inference concerns statutory allocation and design only; it does not indicate prevalence, dominance, actual performance or effectiveness.
- **Boundary:** This does not classify the entire Regulation as one uniform governance logic and does not infer a regulatory regime. The statute does not, by itself, establish the boundaries of the wider institutional system.
