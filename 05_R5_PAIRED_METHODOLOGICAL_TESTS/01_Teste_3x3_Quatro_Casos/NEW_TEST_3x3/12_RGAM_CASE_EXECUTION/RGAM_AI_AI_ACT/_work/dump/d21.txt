ANX11.HEAD	ANNEX XI - Technical documentation referred to in Article 53(1), point (a) — technical documentation for providers of general-purpose AI models
ANX11.S1.HEAD	Section 1 - Information to be provided by all providers of general-purpose AI models
ANX11.S1.P0	The technical documentation referred to in Article 53(1), point (a) shall contain at least the following information as appropriate to the size and risk profile of the model:
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1	A general description of the general-purpose AI model including:
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1.pta	the tasks that the model is intended to perform and the type and nature of AI systems in which it can be integrated;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1.ptb	the acceptable use policies applicable;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1.ptc	the date of release and methods of distribution;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1.ptd	the architecture and number of parameters;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1.pte	the modality (e.g. text, image) and format of inputs and outputs;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt1.ptf	the licence.
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2	A detailed description of the elements of the model referred to in point 1, and relevant information of the process for the development, including the following elements:
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2.pta	the technical means (e.g. instructions of use, infrastructure, tools) required for the general-purpose AI model to be integrated in AI systems;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2.ptb	the design specifications of the model and training process, including training methodologies and techniques, the key design choices including the rationale and assumptions made; what the model is designed to optimise for and the relevance of the different parameters, as applicable;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2.ptc	information on the data used for training, testing and validation, where applicable, including the type and provenance of data and curation methodologies (e.g. cleaning, filtering, etc.), the number of data points, their scope and main characteristics; how the data was obtained and selected as well as all other measures to detect the unsuitability of data sources and methods to detect identifiable biases, where applicable;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2.ptd	the computational resources used to train the model (e.g. number of floating point operations), training time, and other relevant details related to the training;
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2.pte	known or estimated energy consumption of the model.
ANX11.S1.P0.pt2.SP2	With regard to point (e), where the energy consumption of the model is unknown, the energy consumption may be based on information about computational resources used.
ANX11.S2.HEAD	Section 2 - Additional information to be provided by providers of general-purpose AI models with systemic risk
ANX11.S2.P0.pt1	A detailed description of the evaluation strategies, including evaluation results, on the basis of available public evaluation protocols and tools or otherwise of other evaluation methodologies. Evaluation strategies shall include evaluation criteria, metrics and the methodology on the identification of limitations.
ANX11.S2.P0.pt2	Where applicable, a detailed description of the measures put in place for the purpose of conducting internal and/or external adversarial testing (e.g. red teaming), model adaptations, including alignment and fine-tuning.
ANX11.S2.P0.pt3	Where applicable, a detailed description of the system architecture explaining how software components build or feed into each other and integrate into the overall processing.

ANX13.HEAD	ANNEX XIII - Criteria for the designation of general-purpose AI models with systemic risk referred to in Article 51
ANX13.P0	For the purpose of determining that a general-purpose AI model has capabilities or an impact equivalent to those set out in Article 51(1), point (a), the Commission shall take into account the following criteria:
ANX13.P0.pta	the number of parameters of the model;
ANX13.P0.ptb	the quality or size of the data set, for example measured through tokens;
ANX13.P0.ptc	the amount of computation used for training the model, measured in floating point operations or indicated by a combination of other variables such as estimated cost of training, estimated time required for the training, or estimated energy consumption for the training;
ANX13.P0.ptd	the input and output modalities of the model, such as text to text (large language models), text to image, multi-modality, and the state of the art thresholds for determining high-impact capabilities for each modality, and the specific type of inputs and outputs (e.g. biological sequences);
ANX13.P0.pte	the benchmarks and evaluations of capabilities of the model, including considering the number of tasks without additional training, adaptability to learn new, distinct tasks, its level of autonomy and scalability, the tools it has access to;
ANX13.P0.ptf	whether it has a high impact on the internal market due to its reach, which shall be presumed when it has been made available to at least 10 000 registered business users established in the Union;
ANX13.P0.ptg	the number of registered end-users.
