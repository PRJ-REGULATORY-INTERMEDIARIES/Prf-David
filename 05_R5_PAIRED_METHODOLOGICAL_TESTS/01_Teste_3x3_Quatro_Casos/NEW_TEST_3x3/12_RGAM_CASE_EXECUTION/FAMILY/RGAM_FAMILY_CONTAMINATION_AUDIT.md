# RGAM family stage - contamination audit

**Basis:** the four case `ACCESS_AND_CONTAMINATION_LOG.md` files, the case `EXECUTION_PROVENANCE.md` and `RUN_METADATA.csv` files, and the orchestrator's non-substantive `ISOLATION_AUDIT_SUMMARY.md`. The family assembler did not read executor transcripts and did not independently audit tool inputs; every statement about executor access below is attributed to one of those records. The audit claims no more than those records support.

**RGAM_O_DIR_86_361.** The orchestrator summary lists an earlier executor `a86c431b77ac102f0`, interrupted (8/8/9/0/0 tool calls; 0 flagged accesses), which produced the analytical tables and was "later adopted as partial state". ## 4. Cross-case isolation

No case log records access to another case's directory or results; each case log states that no other RGAM case result was read. Executors ran sequentially (blindness policy section 1), and the orchestrator summary states that no case content was passed between executors and that only non-substantive fields were returned. The recovery executors of AI_AI_ACT and O_DIR_86_361 read their own case's recovery note and (for DIR) their own case's earlier tables only.

## 5. Limitations (as recorded; not resolved by this audit)

1. **Technical read-deny was not active.** The blindness policy (section 4) and the orchestrator summary record that the project-level `Read` deny rules were not loaded in the session (a test read of a denied path succeeded) and that they do not block shell commands; the instruction-based allow-list was the primary control.
2. **Shell not blocked.** Executors had Bash/PowerShell access; Python scripts written by executors read allow-listed files but a shell or script could in principle read other paths. The orchestrator audit examined recorded tool inputs (paths and shell command text); the records do not state that the internal behaviour of executor-written scripts was separately inspected.
3. **Audit of tool inputs only.** The orchestrator did not inspect tool results or analytical text; the audit therefore cannot show what an executor saw incidentally in a result.
4. **Executor effort setting not exposed.** The orchestrator summary states that the Agent tool exposes no reasoning-effort parameter. RUN_METADATA of C1_SCF, C2_CBAM and the O_DIR_86_361 recovery run record `reasoning_effort=10` as displayed in the invocation context (the briefs asked for the highest available effort; the displayed value could not be changed); AI_AI_ACT records NOT_EXPOSED.
5. **Executors were not tool-restricted.** Each was a `general-purpose` subagent with Read, Bash, Write, Edit and PowerShell available (per the orchestrator's tool-call counts and the case logs); isolation rested on instructions.
6. **Executor self-reports.** Case access logs, provenance files and RUN_METADATA `files_read` lists are written by the executors themselves; the orchestrator's audit is the only record in the allow-list that is independent of the executors, and it is itself non-substantive and limited as in items 2 and 3.
7. **Interrupted executors.** The earlier executors of AI_AI_ACT and O_DIR_86_361 left no executor-written access log in the case directories; they are covered by the orchestrator audit only. The recovery notes and snapshots (`_RECOVERY_*`) were not read by the family assembler.
8. **Harness-supplied names.** Where disclosed (C1_SCF, C2_CBAM), deny-listed folder names were visible to the executor in its invocation context; the AI_AI_ACT and O_DIR_86_361 logs are silent on this.
9. **Prior knowledge of the models.** The records do not assess whether the executing model had prior knowledge of these public legal instruments or of the experiment's other arms; no claim about this is made here.
10. **Family assembler.** The family assembler's own access is stated in `RGAM_EXECUTION_QA_REPORT.md` section 9: no file content outside the allow-list was read; name-only directory exposures (including the `_RECOVERY_*` folder names and the orchestration log file name) and byte-hashing of manifest-listed files are disclosed there.

## 6. Conclusion limited to the records

Every case access log records status CLEAN; no executor-recorded or orchestrator-flagged read of deny-listed material exists in the records read; flagged items are the authorised recovery notes and one progress-note text, classified benign by the orchestrator. The disclosures and limitations in sections 2 to 5 remain open qualifications on that status.
