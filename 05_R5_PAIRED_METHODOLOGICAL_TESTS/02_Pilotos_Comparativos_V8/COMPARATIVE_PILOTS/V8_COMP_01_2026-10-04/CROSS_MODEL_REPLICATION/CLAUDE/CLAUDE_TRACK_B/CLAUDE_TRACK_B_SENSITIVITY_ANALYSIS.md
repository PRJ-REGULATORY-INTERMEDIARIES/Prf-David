# CLAUDE Track B - O8 Sensitivity

Alternative cuts and readings of the same 20 statements. No statement outside the register was added. For each test: the alternative, the effect on relationships, the effect on configurations, the effect on inferences. Where a test needed a reading of wider text, only the wording that resolves the ambiguity was used and the use is recorded in the log.

The tests were chosen by the analyst from the ambiguities recorded in the uncertainty register; they are therefore not an exhaustive or independent stress test.

## S-01 Split R-P03-01 by object (bodies vs laboratories)

- Alternative: two relations, notification of bodies (CS-P03-001) and information on laboratories (CS-P03-002).
- Relationships: 2 information relations with identical parties instead of 1.
- Configurations: none affected (neither is in a configuration).
- Inferences: none. Relation-level claim survives in both forms.
- Observation: the split adds a record without adding information about the relation (over-segmentation risk). The merged form is retained.

## S-02 Segmentation alternatives

- (a) Merge CS-P03-005 and CS-P03-006 into one statement (the sentence as unit). If relations stay typed, no change. If one undifferentiated relation is forced ("audit and report"), the separate information relation disappears and the stated coupling (the report's object is the audit) becomes internal to one relation: C-P03-A then has one relation and is no longer a configuration. C-P03-A therefore depends on typing the verification and the information relations separately.
- (b) Split R-P04-11 into an adverse/late relation and a positive relation: relations 19 to 20 accepted; C-P04-A core becomes four relations; claim level unchanged.
- (c) Merge CS-P04-001 and CS-P04-002 (one paragraph): no change in relations; C-P04-B unchanged.
- Inferences: no claim level changes. C-P03-A is the claim sensitive to relation typing.

## S-03 Resolve unexpressed actors by context

- (a) CS-P03-004: notifier = notified body (context of the preceding assessment sentence in point 3.3). R-P03-04 would become ACCEPTED at cross_provision_inference (information, with a reasoned-decision content requirement). C-P03-A gains a third member. The contents requirement is a justification component addressed to the audited party; there is still no forum assessing the notified body and no consequence, so accountability stays not established. No gateway: the consequence of the decision is unstated.
- (b) CS-P04-012: submitter of complaints = the complainant (the only party named). R-P04-17 would be accepted for complaints; the submitter of responses stays unexpressed. C-P04-D gains a submission relation consistent with the choice relation.
- Inferences: no claim level changes. Both readings add completeness, not level. Both relations are flagged for human review in the register.

## S-04 Accept designation as a presupposed relation (R-P03-05)

- Alternative: treat "which they have designated" as evidence of a Member State-to-body designation (authorization) relation.
- Relationships: +1 accepted (content-less: what designation confers is unstated in the statements).
- Configurations: X-P03-2 (designation -> notification -> assessment) could be formed if the identity of "notified body" with designated bodies is also accepted (resolved by wider Annex III point 6). C-P03-A would then resemble V8's third-party assurance pattern more closely (recognition of the verifier plus assessment of the target plus reporting back).
- Inferences: still no consequence, supervision or necessity stated; no architecture-level claim; the configuration would remain partial (designation relation without content; the notification to the Commission is not tied to the verification relation by any stated coupling beyond naming).
- Observation: this is the most consequential sensitivity for P03: the strength of the P03 configuration claim depends on whether a presupposed fact in a relative clause counts as a relation.

## S-05 "Independent audit" not identified with "the auditor"

- Alternative: the grammatical subject of CS-P04-001 is an activity and is not an actor; no identification with the auditor of CS-P04-002.
- Relationships: R-P04-01 and R-P04-05 lose their actor A and would be DEFERRED. The statement-level fact that the audit reports to the Commission remains.
- Configurations: C-P04-B would be left with R-P04-02 (appointment) and R-P04-03 (consultation), coupled only by precedence; it would no longer meet the membership criteria for a configuration (the audit and report are what the coupling to the Commission rests on).
- Inferences: C-P04-B does not survive as a configuration under this reading; the claim falls to relation level. This is the single most fragile configuration claim in the track.

## S-06 Remove relations that rest on a condition-embedded actor mention

- Alternative: do not accept R-P04-03 (consultation), R-P04-13 (beneficiary designation), R-P04-16 (complainant choice).
- Relationships: accepted relations across both cases go from 19 to 16 (all three removed are P04).
- Configurations: C-P04-B keeps appointment, audit and report (the Commission's role reduces to report recipient). C-P04-C keeps contract, consent and deposit (coupled by anaphora). C-P04-D loses the relation that carries the cross-reference to the Article 23 list; the remaining two relations (R-P04-19, R-P04-18) would be linked only by shared actor and naming, so C-P04-D would not be a configuration.
- Inferences: C-P04-A, C-P04-B and C-P04-C survive. C-P04-D does not. No architecture-level claim exists to be affected.

## S-07 Readings of "shall give its consent ... before it is concluded" (CS-P04-010)

- Reading 1: the Commission may withhold consent (an approval power over the agreement).
- Reading 2: the Commission is bound to consent when the agreement meets the text's conditions (a formal precondition without discretion).
- Relationships: R-P04-14 is unchanged under both, but its authority and discretion attributes would differ (approval capacity versus formal act).
- Configurations: C-P04-C survives in both readings.
- Inferences: the Commission's position remains a precondition on the conclusion of one agreement. X-P04-3 (supervisory configuration) stays declined in both. The text does not choose between the readings; the mandatory form is compatible with each.

## S-08 Attachment of the manner adverbial in CS-P04-014

- Alternative: "in an objective, transparent and non-discriminatory manner" qualifies the providers' conduct ("reputable bodies with appropriate expertise in ... manner") rather than the Registry's selection.
- Relationships: R-P04-19 unchanged.
- Configurations: C-P04-D unchanged.
- Inferences: only the discretion indicators change (constraints on the Registry's selection reduce to the qualification requirement). No claim level changes.

## S-09 Merge C-P04-A and C-P04-B (audit covers the validation procedure)

- Alternative: treat the audit of the phased registration as covering the validation procedure, forming an assurance-of-assurers configuration.
- Relationships: would require R-P04-25 (audit -> validation agents), which is rejected: no statement connects the audit to the validation agents. The only link is that both concern the phased registration.
- Configurations: the merge is not supported by the 20 statements; X-P04-2 stays declined.
- Inferences: the merge would produce a cross-level claim (verification of those who perform verification) by context alone. It does not survive.

## S-10 Remove the Registry; count-based reading

- Alternative: (a) remove the Registry from the P04 relation set; (b) read the Registry's presence in 11 of 14 P04 statements as centrality.
- Relationships: removing the Registry removes 12 of the 16 accepted P04 relations (those with the Registry as a party: R-P04-02, -03, -05, -06, -07, -11, -12, -13, -14, -15, -18, -19). The remaining four (R-P04-01, -08, -10, -16) form three disconnected pieces: {R-P04-01}, {R-P04-08, R-P04-10}, {R-P04-16}.
- Configurations: the groups fall apart, showing that in the frozen set connectivity runs through the Registry.
- Inferences: this reflects the selection of statements, not a finding (container and frequency errors); removal is not used as an existence criterion (V8 inference rule 7). The count reading is refused (RL-02). No claim relied on it, so no claim changes.

## S-11 Core-only versus Registry-facing attachment in C-P04-A

- Alternative: apply the coupling criteria strictly (no coupling by category naming): C-P04-A = R-P04-08, -10, -11 only; R-P04-06 and R-P04-07 stand as relation-level facts.
- Configurations: the core survives (couplings by stated conditions). The claim that the Registry selects and sets conduct requirements for the examining actor remains at relation level, not at configuration level.
- Inferences: the configuration-level claim shrinks to the applicant-agent-Registry notification sequence; no level change.

## S-12 Annex IV equivalence and anchor shift (P03)

- Alternative: anchor the statements to Annex IV 3.3/4.3 instead of Annex III (identical wording for CS-P03-004, -005, -006; CS-P03-003 differs by a spelling variant).
- Relationships: R-P03-02 and R-P03-03 unchanged. R-P03-04 unchanged (still deferred).
- Configurations: C-P03-A unchanged in content; its scope label changes from "Annex III" to "Annex III or Annex IV wording". Counting verbatim duplicates as separate statements would inflate the evidence; this was not done.
- Inferences: no lift to Art 9 procedures as a whole is gained or lost; RL-06 stands.
