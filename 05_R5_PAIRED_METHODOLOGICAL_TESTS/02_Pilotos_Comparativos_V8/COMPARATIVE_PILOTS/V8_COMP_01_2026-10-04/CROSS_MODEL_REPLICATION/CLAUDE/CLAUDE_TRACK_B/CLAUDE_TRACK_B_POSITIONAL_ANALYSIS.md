# CLAUDE Track B - O5 Position and sequence

Evidence: the 20 frozen statements. Each positional point is labelled:

- **TEXTUAL**: grounded in a modality, condition or temporal marker in a frozen statement (quoted).
- **ENTAILED**: follows from the meaning of a noun, a definite article or a cross-reference but is not stated as a rule. Not a sequence claim.
- **NOT SUPPORTED**: the text does not support a positional claim; none is made.

Definitions used: gateway = a step whose outcome is a stated necessary condition for another actor's right, status or action (needs a stated consequence); order = stated precedence; option = a stated choice or permission; obligatoriness = shall/must; substitutability = a stated alternative actor or path for the same step; conditional authority = a capacity that exists only on a stated condition.

## 1. R-P03-01 (Member States -> Commission, notification) - relation only, no configuration

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| Gateway | NOT SUPPORTED. No statement says that notification is a condition of any body's status or activity |
| Order | ENTAILED: designation is presupposed before notification by the tense of "which they have designated" (CS-P03-001, CS-P03-002). Not a stated rule; designation is not an operative statement here (R-P03-05 DEFERRED) |
| Option | NOT SUPPORTED |
| Obligatoriness | TEXTUAL: "shall notify" (CS-P03-001); "shall inform" (CS-P03-002) |
| Substitutability | NOT SUPPORTED |
| Conditional authority | NOT SUPPORTED. The conditions ("established in the Community", "designated for ...") qualify the object of the duty, not an authority |

## 5. C-P04-C (escrow arrangement, Article 15(1))

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|

TEXTUAL: the Commission's consent is a stated precondition on the conclusion of the escrow agreement ("shall give its consent to that agreement before it is concluded", CS-P04-010). NOT SUPPORTED: that the Registry's operations, the escrow's validity or any other step depend on it; the effect of withholding consent is unstated (U-24) |

| Obligatoriness | TEXTUAL: shall enter into an agreement; shall give consent; shall submit daily (CS-P04-009 to CS-P04-011) |

## 7. Cross-configuration positional observations

- The Registry's selection or appointment relations recur across three configurations (R-P04-02, R-P04-06, R-P04-19), with different modalities and different Commission involvement (consultation in B, none stated in A, none stated in D). The recurrence is recorded as an observation about the 20 statements. It carries no positional claim about the regime: the three steps are not sequenced relative to each other by any statement (X-P04-1, S-10).
- Two Commission positions in the P04 statements are textually distinct and should not be merged: consulted before the appointment of the auditor (CS-P04-002), and consenting before conclusion of the agreement (CS-P04-010). The text gives the second an express precedence marker and the word "consent", and the first only "after consulting".

## 8. Points where positional claims were refused (summary)

1. Gateway for the notified body's assessment, audit or decision (C-P03-A).
2. Gateway for the validation agent's finding (C-P04-A).
3. Gateway for the audit (C-P04-B) and for the escrow arrangement beyond the conclusion of the agreement (C-P04-C).
4. Order between assessment and surveillance (C-P03-A) and between selection/requirement and the validation steps (C-P04-A).
5. Substitutability of validation agents, auditors, escrow agents (as to path) and notified bodies.
6. Whether consultation constrains appointment (C-P04-B) and whether consent may be withheld (C-P04-C).

Result of O5: positional content is thin. The operation separated stated precedence from entailed order and consultation from consent, and recorded a dependency of an option set on a permissive power; it supported no gateway, substitutability or conditional-authority claim at configuration level.
