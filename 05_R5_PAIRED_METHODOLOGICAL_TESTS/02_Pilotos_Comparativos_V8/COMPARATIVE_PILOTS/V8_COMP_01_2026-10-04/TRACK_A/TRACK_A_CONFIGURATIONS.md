# Track A — V8 configurations

## Scope and method

This is a prospective V8 relational-leximetrics reconstruction using the frozen 20-statement input only. A configuration is stated only where a bounded pattern of functions, actors, relationships, and instruments is evidenced. The analysis concerns **formal legal architecture**, not implementation, operation, the complete instrument, or a regulatory regime.

## P03 — Directive 91/263/EEC

### TA-P03-CFG-01 — vertical designation-information configuration

- **Problem/function:** make designated conformity-related bodies and test laboratories legible to the Commission.
- **Relations:** R-P03-001 and R-P03-002, both mandatory information relations from Member States to the Commission.
- **Elements/instruments:** designated bodies, test laboratories, identifying symbols, notification/information duties.
- **Attributes:** binding information duties. No Commission assessment, consequence, or accountability chain is stated.
- **Boundary:** Article 10(1)–(2) only. Designation is an object/condition in these statements, not a separately reconstructed relation.
- **Claim:** a narrow vertical information configuration is supported.

### TA-P03-CFG-02 — quality-system assurance configuration

- **Problem/function:** assess and monitor maintenance/application of a manufacturer’s quality system.
- **Relations/elements:** R-P03-003 (notified-body audit of manufacturer), R-P03-004 (audit report to manufacturer), and the retained quality-system assessment assignment in CS-P03-003.
- **Instrument/mechanism:** quality-system assessment and periodic audit/reporting.
- **Attributes:** the notified body has a limited, function-specific audit capacity. An accountability system is not inferred: the selected text does not establish a forum’s assessment, explanation, review, or consequence.
- **Boundary:** Annex III points 3.3 and 4.3 as represented in the freeze. The passive decision-notification statement (CS-P03-004) remains outside the configuration because its sender is unknown.
- **Claim:** a bounded formal assurance configuration is supported.

### P03 configuration conclusion

The two configurations are legally observable but are not connected by an evidenced coupling in the frozen microcase. Accordingly, Track A does **not** infer a unitary P03 governance architecture. The stopping rule is applied at configuration level.

## P04 — Regulation (EC) No 874/2004

### TA-P04-CFG-01 — auditor appointment and consultative component

- **Problem/function:** constitute an independent audit at the end of phased registration.
- **Relations/elements:** R-P04-001 (Registry appoints auditor) and R-P04-002 (Registry consults Commission); CS-P04-001 retains the audit-report requirement as an assignment rather than adding an unsupported auditor-to-Commission edge.
- **Attributes:** Registry has appointment capacity; the Commission’s consultation does not establish approval, veto, accountability, or continuing supervision.
- **Boundary:** Article 12(5) only.
- **Claim:** a narrow appointment/consultation component is supported; a complete audit-accountability configuration is not.

### TA-P04-CFG-02 — delegated prior-right validation configuration

- **Problem/function:** receive, examine, and communicate the status of claimed prior rights.
- **Relations:** R-P04-003 (Registry selects validation agents), R-P04-004 (Registry requires validation execution), R-P04-005 (applicant submits evidence), R-P04-006 (agent examines applicant claim), and R-P04-007 (agent informs Registry under positive/negative conditions).
- **Elements/instruments:** validation agents, documentary evidence, first-in-line procedure, evidence and validation procedure.
- **Attributes:** Registry has function-specific selection/requirement capacities; agents have limited assessment capacity. The configuration supports a distribution of validation work, not a finding about actual outcomes or system-wide intermediary centrality.
- **Boundary:** Articles 13–14 as represented by CS-P04-003 to CS-P04-008. No Registry disposition after notification is added.
- **Claim:** a formal delegated validation configuration is supported.

### TA-P04-CFG-03 — conditional escrow-continuity configuration

- **Problem/function:** arrange continuity-preserving custody of a current database copy.
- **Relations:** R-P04-008 (Registry–escrow agreement), R-P04-009 (Commission’s prior consent condition), R-P04-010 (Registry’s daily database submission).
- **Elements/instruments:** escrow agreement, trustee/escrow agent, Commission beneficiary designation, daily copy.
- **Attributes:** Registry’s agreement-forming capacity and the Commission’s agreement-specific consent capacity are both limited to the stated arrangement. No broader supervisory relationship is inferred.
- **Boundary:** Article 15(1) only.
- **Claim:** a formal conditional escrow-continuity configuration is supported.

### TA-P04-CFG-04 — partial ADR-provider interface configuration

- **Problem/function:** constitute a selectable ADR-provider pool and communicate a properly filed ADR request to the Registry.
- **Relations:** R-P04-011 (ADR provider informs Registry) and R-P04-012 (Registry may select ADR providers).
- **Elements/instruments:** ADR provider list/selection, ADR filing event.
- **Boundary:** Articles 22(5)–(6) and 23(1) as frozen. CS-P04-012 creates no submitter-to-provider relation because the submitter is unexpressed; Article 22(7) is excluded.
- **Claim:** a partial formal interface configuration is supported, not a complete ADR adjudication configuration.

### P04 configuration conclusion

The four bounded configurations share a Registry-centered allocation of appointment, selection, requirement, agreement, submission, and provider-selection functions. That patterned coupling supports the limited architecture claim documented in `TRACK_A_ARCHITECTURAL_INFERENCES.md`. It does not establish a complete architecture of the whole regulation, implemented governance, or any regulatory regime.
