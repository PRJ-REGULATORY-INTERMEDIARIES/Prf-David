# Track A — V8 architectural inferences

## Baseline and inferential rule

Track A applies David Levi-Faur’s V8 framework dated 03 October 2026, operationalized only through the frozen V8 codebook, inference rules, and relational model named in the Track A freeze manifest. It does **not** use the additional eight-operation protocol.

V8’s vertical traceability requirement is applied as:

`architectural claim → bounded configuration → qualified relationship/element → common statement → official legal HTML/version`.

The claims below concern only the formal legal arrangements observable in this frozen microcase evidence. They are not findings about the full legal instrument, current law, implementation, operation, or regulatory regime.

## P03

### TA-P03-AI-01 — no unitary architecture claim in the frozen scope

- **Claim level:** configuration (negative stopping decision), not architecture.
- **Evidence chain:** TA-P03-CFG-01 (Member State–Commission information relations R-P03-001/002) and TA-P03-CFG-02 (notified-body–manufacturer quality-system assurance relations R-P03-003/004 plus CS-P03-003).
- **Reason:** each bounded configuration is supported, but the selected statements do not establish the coupling by which they would constitute a single architectural arrangement. Shared membership in the Directive and the word “designated” are insufficient under V8.
- **Alternative considered and rejected:** infer a centrally coordinated certification architecture joining Member States, Commission, notified bodies, and manufacturers. Rejected because the frozen evidence provides notification to the Commission and quality-system assurance, but no legally evidenced relation linking those components.
- **Uncertainty:** CS-P03-004 has no expressed sender; any relational use of it would add an actor.

### P03 principal reconstruction

P03 contains a **vertical designation-information configuration** and a **quality-system assurance configuration**. In this bounded sample, their coexistence does not support a whole-microcase architecture claim. This is a substantive stopping-rule result, not absence of governance content.

## P04

### TA-P04-AI-01 — Registry-centered formal multi-actor administrative architecture, bounded to the selected microcase

- **Claim level:** architecture, `cross_provision_inference`.
- **Problem/scale:** administration of phased registration, prior-right validation, continuity arrangement, and the limited ADR interface within the selected provisions.
- **Configurations:** TA-P04-CFG-01 (auditor appointment/consultation), CFG-02 (delegated validation), CFG-03 (conditional escrow continuity), and CFG-04 (partial ADR-provider interface).
- **Qualified coupling:** the Registry is the expressly identified party in appointment, consultation, validation-agent selection and requirement, escrow agreement, daily submission, and ADR-provider selection. The Commission has narrowly specified consultative/consent roles; external validation agents, escrow agents, auditors, and ADR providers perform differentiated functions.
- **Attributes:** Registry’s capacities are function-specific rather than generalized; validation agents have limited assessment function; Commission consent is agreement-specific. Accountability is not promoted from reporting/information alone.
- **Architectural significance:** Registry centrality is supported by direct, functionally diverse, and repeated legal relations across the bounded configurations, not merely a mention count. No claim is made about system-wide intermediary centrality, because indispensability, substitutability, and the omitted portions of the Regulation are not evidenced in this freeze.
- **Boundary and alternative:** the claim is limited to the frozen P04 microcase. A competing reading—separate unconnected administrative fragments—was assessed. It is weaker because the Registry directly connects the configurations through multiple legal capacities and instruments. The broader reading—architecture of the entire .eu domain regime—is rejected as beyond the sample.

### P04 principal reconstruction

Within the ten authorized anchors, P04 formally distributes administrative work through a Registry-centered set of relationships with specialized external actors, subject to narrowly specified Commission involvement. The claim remains a **formal, bounded architecture**; it makes no implementation, performance, or regime claim.

## Cross-case discipline

The different stopping points are evidence-led: P03 stops at two configurations; P04 supports one bounded architecture. This is not an A/B comparison and is not a claim that either instrument is generally more architectural. Both cases retain their uncertainties in `TRACK_A_UNCERTAINTY_REGISTER.csv`.
