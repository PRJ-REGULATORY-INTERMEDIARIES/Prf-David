# Track B — positional analysis (O5)

Positional claims concern an actor or relation’s place in a specified arrangement. They are not inferred from occurrence counts or graph reachability alone.

## P03

| Candidate position | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Commission as a governing gateway | Receives two mandatory information flows. | Rejected. The frozen evidence gives neither assessment, authorization, consequence nor a decision sequence. It is an information recipient in TB-P03-CFG-01 only. |
| Notified body as an assurance gateway | Audits and reports to manufacturer. | Rejected as a gateway claim. The evidence supports a bounded audit/report role, but no legal dependency, market-access consequence or non-substitutable sequence. |
| Member States as vertical information source | Two mandatory duties to Commission. | Accepted as a situated source position in TB-P03-CFG-01, not as a whole-architecture property. |

## P04

| Candidate position | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Validation agent as procedural assessment gateway | Applicant shall submit documentary evidence to an agent; agent shall examine and notify Registry under both positive and negative conditions (CS-P04-005–008). | Accepted, but narrowly: the agent is a necessary stage in the frozen evidence-to-Registry notification sequence. No claim is made about final domain-name allocation, system-wide intermediation, or non-substitutability among agents. |
| Registry as coordination hub | Registry appoints, consults, selects, requires, contracts, submits, and may select across four bounded configurations. | Accepted only within the selected P04 microcase. The position arises from cross-configuration functional connection, not frequency alone. No whole-Regulation centrality metric is claimed. |
| Commission as escrow-specific approval gate | Consent is required before the stated escrow agreement is concluded. | Accepted as a relation-specific gate in TB-P04-CFG-03. It does not establish general supervision, veto authority outside this agreement, or a system-wide vertical hierarchy. |
| ADR provider as adjudicative gateway | Provider informs Registry after filing; submission actor and Article 22(7) are unavailable. | Rejected. The evidence supports an interface role, not a completed adjudicative path or gateway status. |

## Result

O5 adds bounded positional clarification for P04: the validation agent occupies a necessary **procedural assessment** stage, the Registry a microcase-level coordination position, and Commission consent a local agreement gate. It adds no positional claim to P03 beyond its two configuration-specific roles.
