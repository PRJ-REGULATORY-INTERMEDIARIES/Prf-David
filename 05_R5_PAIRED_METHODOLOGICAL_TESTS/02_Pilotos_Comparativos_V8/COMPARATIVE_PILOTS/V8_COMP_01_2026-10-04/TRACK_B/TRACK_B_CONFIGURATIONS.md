# Track B — configurational reconstruction (O4)

This reconstruction uses only the frozen common statements. A configuration is a bounded combination of qualified relationships, functions, mechanisms, instruments and attributes organized around a stated problem or stage. Overlap does not itself establish a higher-order architecture.

## P03

### TB-P03-CFG-01 — designated-actor information configuration

- **Problem/stage:** make the designated bodies, symbols and test laboratories relevant to Article 9 procedures known to the Commission.
- **Membership rule:** binding information relations whose sender is a Member State and recipient is the Commission.
- **Relations/evidence:** TB-R-P03-001 and TB-R-P03-002, from CS-P03-001 and CS-P03-002.
- **Function/mechanism/instrument:** information; notification/information; notification duties.
- **Coupling:** same vertical sender-recipient pattern with distinct designated object categories.
- **Boundary:** no state-to-body designation relation is inferred; no Commission assessment, follow-up or consequence is evidenced.

### TB-P03-CFG-02 — quality-system assurance configuration

- **Problem/stage:** assess, monitor and communicate matters concerning a manufacturer’s quality system.
- **Membership rule:** quality-system assurance work performed by the notified body in respect of the manufacturer.
- **Relations/evidence:** TB-R-P03-006 (periodic audit) and TB-R-P03-007 (audit report), together with retained assignment CS-P03-003.
- **Function/mechanism/instrument:** verification/assurance and information; assessment, audit and reporting; quality-system assessment/audit report.
- **Coupling:** audit produces a report to the same manufacturer; the assessment assignment supplies the related assurance purpose but does not add an actor edge.
- **Boundary:** CS-P03-004 is not included because the notifying actor is unspecified. No certification, sanction, market-access or accountability consequence is added.

### P03 result

Two configurations are reconstructed. Their placement in the same Directive is not a qualified coupling. No third configuration or whole-microcase architecture is created.

## P04

### TB-P04-CFG-01 — independent-audit appointment and consultation configuration

- **Problem/stage:** constitute an independent audit at the end of phased registration.
- **Membership rule:** relations governing auditor appointment and the required consultative step.
- **Relations/evidence:** TB-R-P04-002 (Registry appoints auditor) and TB-R-P04-003 (Registry consults Commission), with CS-P04-001 retained as an audit-report assignment.
- **Function/mechanism/instrument:** authorization/coordination; appointment and consultation; independent audit.
- **Boundary:** no actor-to-Commission audit-report relation, Commission approval, or audit accountability system is inferred.

### TB-P04-CFG-02 — delegated documentary validation configuration

- **Problem/stage:** receive evidence of a claimed prior right, assess it, and transmit the resulting status to the Registry.
- **Membership rule:** legally required relations around validation-agent selection, required validation, evidence submission, examination, and conditional notice.
- **Relations/evidence:** TB-R-P04-004 through TB-R-P04-009 from CS-P04-003 through CS-P04-008.
- **Function/mechanism/instrument:** selection/oversight, evidence submission, verification/assurance and information; objective selection, required validation, documentary examination and conditional notification; validation arrangement/procedure.
- **Coupling:** the applicant must submit evidence to a validation agent; the agent examines the claim and must notify the Registry for both negative and positive conditional outcomes. Registry selection/requirement frames the agent role.
- **Boundary:** no conclusion is drawn about Registry’s final disposition, legal effect of an agent finding, or actual performance.

### TB-P04-CFG-03 — conditional escrow-continuity configuration

- **Problem/stage:** secure a current electronic copy of the `.eu` database through an escrow arrangement.
- **Membership rule:** relations constituting, conditioning and operating the stated escrow instrument.
- **Relations/evidence:** TB-R-P04-010 (Registry agreement), TB-R-P04-012 (Commission consent condition), and TB-R-P04-013 (daily submission).
- **Function/mechanism/instrument:** continuity arrangement and information; contractual organization, prior consent and daily submission; escrow agreement/arrangement.
- **Coupling:** the agreement must exist, Commission consent must precede conclusion, and the Registry must then submit a daily copy to the agent.
- **Boundary:** beneficiary designation remains a condition, not an independent Registry–Commission relation; no operational custody or recovery outcome is inferred.

### TB-P04-CFG-04 — partial ADR-provider interface configuration

- **Problem/stage:** create a selectable ADR-provider pool and require communication of a properly filed ADR request to the Registry.
- **Membership rule:** Registry provider-selection capacity plus provider-to-Registry information relation.
- **Relations/evidence:** TB-R-P04-015 and TB-R-P04-016 from CS-P04-013 and CS-P04-014; CS-P04-012 retained as a non-relational procedural assignment.
- **Function/mechanism/instrument:** authorization/selection and information; permitted selection and conditional notice; ADR procedure.
- **Boundary:** Article 22(7) is excluded. The frozen text does not express the complaint/response submitter, therefore this is not a complete adjudication configuration.

### P04 result

The four configurations are complementary but not mutually exclusive. Their qualified couplings and positional consequences are considered in O5–O7; shared Registry membership alone is not the warrant for an architecture claim.
