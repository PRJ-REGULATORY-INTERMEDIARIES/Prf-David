# Track B — architectural inference (O7)

## Method and claim boundary

Track B applies V8 together with the project’s additional eight-operation procedural protocol. V8 supplies the concepts of relationships, configurations, architectural significance, vertical traceability and stopping. The additional protocol supplies an explicit sequence for delimitation, decomposition, relation reconstruction, configuration, position, part–whole attribution, warrant, and sensitivity. It is not attributed to V8 as a conceptual innovation.

All claims concern the **formal legal arrangements constituted in the frozen microcases**. They do not claim present legal force, implementation, operational effects, complete instruments, or regulatory regimes.

## P03

### TB-P03-AI-01 — architecture inference stopped at configuration level

- **Problem and scale:** selected information and quality-system assurance provisions only.
- **Configurations:** TB-P03-CFG-01 (designated-actor information) and TB-P03-CFG-02 (quality-system assurance).
- **Qualified coupling test:** no frozen statement establishes a relationship, dependency, shared procedure, authority sequence, or legal consequence connecting these two configurations.
- **Positional/part–whole test:** the Commission is an information recipient; the notified body is an auditor/reporter. Neither role proves a whole-level gateway or architecture.
- **Alternative/boundary test:** treating designation or passive notification differently does not generate the missing coupling.
- **Conclusion:** P03 supports two bounded formal configurations, but **no unitary governance architecture claim** under the authorized microcase boundary.

## P04

### TB-P04-AI-01 — bounded Registry-coordinated multi-actor administrative architecture

- **Problem and scale:** selected legal arrangements for audit appointment, prior-right validation, escrow continuity and the limited ADR-provider interface.
- **Configurations:** TB-P04-CFG-01 (audit appointment/consultation), TB-P04-CFG-02 (delegated documentary validation), TB-P04-CFG-03 (conditional escrow continuity), and TB-P04-CFG-04 (partial ADR-provider interface).
- **Qualified coupling:** Registry relations provide the connecting legal capacities across the selected configurations: appointment, mandatory consultation, validation-agent selection and requirement, escrow agreement, daily information submission, and ADR-provider selection. The configurations use distinct instruments but connect through expressly allocated Registry functions.
- **Configurational consequence:** governance work is formally distributed to specialized external actors while the Registry holds bounded coordinating capacities. Validation agents occupy a necessary procedural assessment stage in the evidence-to-Registry notification sequence; Commission consent is a local condition for escrow agreement formation.
- **Architectural significance:** the claim rests on functional breadth, authority-bearing selection/requirement/appointment powers, conditional sequence, and cross-configuration connection. It does not rest on actor mentions, graph connectivity or a centrality score.
- **Part–whole control:** Registry capacities belong first to particular relations; the coordination description belongs only to this authorized configuration set. The validation-agent gateway is a positional property of the validation configuration, not a claim of system-wide intermediary centrality.
- **Alternatives and sensitivity:** omitting Commission consent removes the escrow-specific gate but not the remaining bounded architecture. Omitting the incomplete ADR interface narrows but does not eliminate the claim. A validation-only boundary supports a configuration, not the multi-configuration architecture.
- **Conclusion:** the evidence supports a **formal, bounded Registry-coordinated multi-actor administrative architecture** in the selected P04 microcase. It does not support a claim about the complete Regulation, its implementation, or a regulatory regime.
