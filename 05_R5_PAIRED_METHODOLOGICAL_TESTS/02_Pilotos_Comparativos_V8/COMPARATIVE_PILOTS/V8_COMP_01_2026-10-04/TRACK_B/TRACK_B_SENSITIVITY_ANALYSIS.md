# Track B — boundary and sensitivity analysis (O8)

Sensitivity asks whether a credible alternative interpretation or boundary changes the claim. It is not a mechanical removal test.

## P03

| Alternative | Effect | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Treat the designation language in CS-P03-001/002 as a separate Member State–body relationship. | Adds an uncertain designation candidate but does not establish a qualified coupling with the manufacturer-facing assurance configuration. | P03 still stops at two configurations. |
| Treat the notifier in CS-P03-004 as the notified body. | Could add a manufacturer-facing notification relation, but the actor is not stated. | Not adopted; even if adopted, it would not connect the Commission information configuration to the assurance configuration. |
| Narrow the evidence to only Article 10 or only Annex III. | Leaves one configuration respectively. | Confirms a unitary P03 architecture depends on an unsupported combination and is not inferred. |

## P04

| Alternative | Effect | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Merge positive and negative validation notices into one relation. | Preserves the validation configuration but loses the legally relevant directional conditions. | Keep two relations for traceable conditional contrast; configuration conclusion is stable. |
| Remove the Commission consent relation because ‘that agreement’ is treated as too ambiguous. | The escrow configuration retains Registry–agent agreement and daily submission; its local approval-gate feature disappears. | P04 bounded architecture remains supported by validation/escrow/appointment components, but Commission’s local position is withdrawn. |
| Exclude partial ADR-provider interface because Article 22(7) is unavailable. | Removes one incomplete configuration. | Registry-centered bounded architecture is narrower but remains supported by appointment, validation, and escrow components. |
| Apply a validation-only boundary. | Supports a delegated validation configuration and bounded validation-agent assessment gateway, but not a multi-configuration P04 architecture. | The architecture claim is boundary-sensitive and must remain explicitly tied to the 14-statement microcase. |
| Treat applicant as complaint submitter in CS-P04-012. | Would add an unsupported ADR entry relation. | Not adopted; partial ADR status remains. |

## Sensitivity conclusion

P03’s stopping decision is robust across credible alternatives. P04’s delegated validation configuration is robust, while its architecture-level claim depends on the authorized multi-configuration boundary. This does not invalidate the bounded P04 claim; it limits its scale and rules out whole-instrument generalization.
