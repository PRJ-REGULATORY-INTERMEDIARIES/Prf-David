# Track B — part–whole analysis (O6)

## Property-bearer discipline

| Case | Observation | Proper bearer | Prohibited generalization |
|---|---|---|---|
| P03 | Member States must notify/inform Commission. | Each specified Member State–Commission information relation. | Commission-wide authority, accountability system, or entire-Directive hierarchy. |
| P03 | Notified body audits manufacturer’s quality-system practice. | Notified-body/manufacturer audit relation and TB-P03-CFG-02. | Whole-Directive intermediary centrality or certification architecture. |
| P03 | Audit report is provided to manufacturer. | Information relation TB-R-P03-007. | Accountability relation: no forum assessment or consequence is stated. |
| P04 | Registry appoints/selects/requires. | Registry’s function-specific capacities in the cited relations. | Unconstrained general authority or dominance over the entire Regulation. |
| P04 | Validation agent receives evidence, assesses claim and notifies Registry. | Agent’s relation-specific assessment function and procedural position in TB-P04-CFG-02. | Final allocation authority or system-wide intermediary centrality. |
| P04 | Commission consents before escrow agreement. | Commission–Registry-agreement consent relation and local gate position. | General supervisory authority or full accountability system. |
| P04 | Registry connects four selected configurations. | The bounded P04 configuration set in this microcase. | Complete Regulation, implemented governance, operational architecture or a regime. |

## Result

The extra procedural discipline yields a specific safeguard: it separates (a) actor capacities, (b) relationship attributes, (c) positional properties, and (d) the limited whole-level property of the selected configuration set. It does not create new legal content. It prevents the selected P04 architecture claim from being reported as a property of every Registry function or of the Regulation as a whole.
