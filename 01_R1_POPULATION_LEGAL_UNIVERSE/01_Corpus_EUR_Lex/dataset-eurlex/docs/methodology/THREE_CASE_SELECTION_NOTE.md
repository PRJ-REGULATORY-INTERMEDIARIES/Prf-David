# Three-case selection note

## Purpose

The production set contains three EU legislative acts to support a small comparative exercise on regulatory architectures of the Union's green transition. Three cases allow the existing framework case to be read alongside two materially different implementation architectures while keeping complete corpus reading and auditability manageable.

## Selection criteria

Cases were selected prospectively for: relevance to the EU green transition; substantive regulatory and institutional content; complementarity with Case 01; diversity of regulatory architecture; official EUR-Lex availability; manageable complete reading; and temporal relevance. Official source availability was mandatory. Selection did not use intermediary expectations, R-I-T results, keyword-hit counts, or prior empirical outputs.

## Cases

| Case | CELEX | Act | Regulatory architecture | Selection rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Case 01 | 32021R1119 | Regulation (EU) 2021/1119 — European Climate Law | Framework and climate governance | Existing locked pilot case retained as the framework baseline. |
| Case 02 | 32023R0956 | Regulation (EU) 2023/956 — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism | Cross-border market regulation, emissions accounting, certificates, customs coordination, enforcement and delegated implementation | Adds a bounded market/compliance architecture directly connected to climate transition and carbon leakage. |
| Case 03 | 32023R1115 | Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 — Deforestation-Free Products Regulation | Supply-chain due diligence, risk assessment, traceability, information governance, market access and supervision | Adds a distinct product-chain environmental compliance architecture connected to emissions and biodiversity. |

## Selection safeguard

Was either act selected because it was already known to contain an R-I-T intermediary relationship? **NO.** Neither new case was selected using knowledge of intermediary relationships, R-I-T results, keyword hits, or prior model outputs.

## Status

The three canonical corpora are frozen and no empirical coding has started. Case 02 and Case 03 use the original English legal acts identified by CELEX and preserved from official EUR-Lex XHTML. Case 01 remains unchanged.
