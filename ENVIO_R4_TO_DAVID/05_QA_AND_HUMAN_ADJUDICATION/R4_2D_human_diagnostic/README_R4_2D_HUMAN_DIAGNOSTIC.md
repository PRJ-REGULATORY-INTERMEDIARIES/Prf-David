# R4.2D Human diagnostic

Open `R4_2D_HUMAN_DIAGNOSTIC_PANEL.html` locally in a modern browser. The panel has no external resources. It presents one case at a time: frozen full legal text, original Luna fields, then labeled Luna/Terra diagnostic evidence, followed by researcher decisions.

Review the source text directly. `NONE_EXPLICIT` is permitted for counterpart and recipient when the provision expresses neither relation. For passive structures, do not invent an actor; `ACTOR_NOT_EXPLICIT` may be entered in the correction field. Do not assign regulator/intermediary/target roles or code mechanisms.

The panel stores answers in browser local storage. It marks a record `REVIEWED` only after a human pattern decision and all five separate field decisions have been selected. Export CSV and JSON after review; preserve both exports.

The 12-row sample uses Priority 1 paired-model cases, with two cases per pattern and act. It is purposive and diagnoses whether the patterns occur; it cannot estimate prevalence in the 1,404-row corpus. No human decisions are prefilled.
