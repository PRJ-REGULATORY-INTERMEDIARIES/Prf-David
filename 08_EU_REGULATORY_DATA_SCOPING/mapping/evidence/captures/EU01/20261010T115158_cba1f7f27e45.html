    Help to search for the document year in the ‘find results by document number’ widget.
    Help to enter the act number in the ‘find results by document number’ widget. Example: 1036 for Regulation (EU) 2016/1036, or 725 for Communication COM (2016) 0725. <a href="../content/help/eurlex-content/numbering-of-acts.html" target="_blank">Read more on numbering of acts</a>.
    Help to choose the type of document in the ‘find results by document number widget’.
    Help to enter the CELEX number in the ‘find results by CELEX number’ widget. The CELEX number is the Uunique identifier of a document on EUR-Lex (e.g. 32006L0121). You can also enter partial numbers, replacing the missing characters with wildcards: * for multiple characters, ? for a single character. <a href="../content/help/eurlex-content/celex-number.html" target="_blank">More about CELEX numbers</a>.
