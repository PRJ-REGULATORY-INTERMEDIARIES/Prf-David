Stories from the ground

Posting of third-country nationals in the EU

Workers from outside the EU play a vital role in Europe’s economy, often as posted workers moving between Member States. 

In 2025 ELA has published a study shedding light on their working conditions, drawing on first-hand accounts from third-country nationals and national authorities. 

The findings highlight persistent gaps in protection and the need for stronger safeguards.
