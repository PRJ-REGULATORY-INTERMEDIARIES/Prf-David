About EU Vocabularies

The EU Vocabularies website is your gateway to accessing a wide range of multilingual reference data assets published by the Publications Office of the European Union. Our platform hosts a diverse collection of semantic assets, including multilingual corporate reference data assets, code lists, authority lists, taxonomies, thesauri, glossaries and ontologies, used by various EU institutions and agencies.

Explore our resources to enhance your data management processes and contribute to a more interconnected digital environment across Europe. For further collaboration or specific needs, please contact us to discuss potential solutions.
