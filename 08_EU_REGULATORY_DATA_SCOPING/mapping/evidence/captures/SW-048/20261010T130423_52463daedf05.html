Annual Report

The Court of Justice of the European Union publishes two key reports each year: the Year in Review and the Management report. Both reports are available online, offering insights into the year’s leading cases and the Court’s financial management respectively.

The Year in Review

The Year in Review is the annual report of the Court. It presents the Court’s activities and the most significant case-law of the Court of Justice and the General Court from the past year.

Each edition contains an overview of the key events of the year, statistics on the work of the two courts and a “Case-law focus” section offering clear and comprehensive reviews of the year’s major cases. The report also features contributions from the Presidents of both courts and the Registrar of the Court of Justice, reflecting on the developments and achievements of each court and the institution as a whole.

 

Management Report

The Management Report is the annual activity report that shows how the Court manages its resources. It is submitted to the EU’s budgetary authorities in accordance with the EU’s financial rules. The report highlights the results achieved by the Court using the resources it has been allocated and evaluates how the internal control systems function.
