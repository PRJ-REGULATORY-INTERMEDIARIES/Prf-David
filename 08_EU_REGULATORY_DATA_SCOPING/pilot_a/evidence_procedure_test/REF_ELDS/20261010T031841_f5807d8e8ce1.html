Principles

The European legal data space (ELDS) is one of the Common European Data Spaces designated to harness the value of data for the benefit of the European economy and society, as set out in the European strategy for data of February 2020. 

In line with priorities highlighted by the European strategy for data for the common European data spaces for public administration, the European legal data space aims at providing easily accessible, reusable, interoperable data in the area of legislation and case-law across the EU.

This data can be used in decision-making, research and development of innovative legal tools. The legal data space will support public administrations, legal practitioners, and society in general.

1. Common standards

The following links provide guidance and common standards for promoting interoperable frameworks and re-use of legal information from Member States and the EU:

  • Link to information about the European Legislation Identifier (ELI): About ELI - EUR-Lex
  • Link to information about the European Court Law Identifier (ECLI): European e-Justice Portal - European Case Law Identifier (ECLI)

2. Access to collections of legislation and case-law

  • List of EU and national legal depositories: https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/elds
  • Machine access to EUR-Lex content: Reuse EUR-Lex content - EUR-Lex
  • List of EU and national case-law portals: https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/elds-caselaw
  • ECLI search: European Case Law Identifier (ECLI) search engine | European e-Justice Portal
  • Prototype for a search using ELI: ELI search

3. Data on justice systems and improving access to justice

Electronic one stop shop on all justice matters providing information on justice systems and improving access to justice:

  • European e-Justice Portal: Home | European e-Justice Portal

4. EUR-Lex data dump

It allows downloading in bulk all legal acts in force per language (CELEX sector 3).

To access the data dump an EU login account is required.

Link to EUR-Lex data dump: https://datadump.publications.europa.eu/
