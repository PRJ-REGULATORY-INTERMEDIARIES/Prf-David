# Concept note

**DRAFT — NOT VALIDATED · version 0.1.0**

The proposed Regulatory Intermediaries Observatory asks how regulatory intermediaries, their functions and their relations with regulators and regulatory targets can be identified, verified and compared. Its conceptual starting point is the Regulator–Intermediary–Target model discussed in the supplied project specification. Bibliographic leads remain subject to verification.

Track A observes datasets, portals, APIs and documentary collections. It records potential analytical usefulness; this is not evidence of an intermediary. Track B observes bounded regulatory episodes, identifying actors and demonstrating contextual roles and directed relationships through documentary evidence. An organization is not intrinsically an intermediary.

Questions: Which sources can support explicit or inferential identification? Which functions, instruments and relationships are recoverable? What information is lost in organization-only records? What evidence, uncertainty and review conditions make a record reusable?

EU sources are an initial proposal, not an approved restriction. Geographic, temporal, linguistic and sectoral boundaries remain open. The suggested twelve-episode, three-domain pilot is a hypothesis for exercising coding rules, not a representative sample or commitment. No empirical conclusions or prevalence estimates can be drawn from this infrastructure.

The observatory has no confirmed institutional endorsement, team membership or publication mandate. RGAM/V8 data, ontologies and protocols are outside this implementation.
