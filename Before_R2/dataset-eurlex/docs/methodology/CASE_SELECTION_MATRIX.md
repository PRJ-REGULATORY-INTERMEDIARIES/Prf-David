## Selection decision

Case 02 is **Regulation (EU) 2023/956**, the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism. Case 03 is **Regulation (EU) 2023/1115**, the Deforestation-Free Products Regulation. Together with Case 01, they provide the most informative and manageable contrast in this shortlist: climate framework/governance, cross-border market/compliance regulation, and supply-chain due-diligence/information governance. The pair also avoids relying on a single product-standard, infrastructure, lifecycle, or financial-market architecture.

The decision was made from the criteria above, official source accessibility, complete-reading scope, and institutional diversity. It was not made from R-I-T coding, intermediary expectations, previous model outputs, keyword-hit counts, or expected positives.

## Selection safeguard

**Was either act selected because it was already known to contain an R-I-T intermediary relationship? NO.** The selection preceded coding and did not use empirical results or prior knowledge of intermediary relationships.
